# Starter voice sources — provenance and legal basis

Every text bundled here is in the **public domain in the United States**
(published before 1930; both authors died more than 70 years ago), so
Anjadhe may redistribute them and users may do anything with them.
Project Gutenberg headers/branding are stripped per their license (the
underlying texts carry no restrictions); punctuation lightly normalized.

## Mark Twain (Samuel Clemens, 1835–1910)
From *Mark Twain's Speeches* (1910), via Project Gutenberg ebook #3188:
- The Weather (1876)
- The Babies (1879)
- Unconscious Plagiarism (1879)
- Advice to Girls (1868)

## Abraham Lincoln (1809–1865)
- The Gettysburg Address (1863) — via Project Gutenberg ebook #4,
  transcription ellipses normalized to dashes.
- Second Inaugural Address (1865) — via Wikisource (from *Life and Works
  of Abraham Lincoln*); also a U.S. government work (17 U.S.C. § 105).

Do NOT add texts here without verifying public-domain status — "publicly
available" is not "public domain", and translations carry their own
copyright. Living or recently deceased people are additionally off-limits
as SHIPPED voices for right-of-publicity reasons: users may create such
voices themselves from content they have, but Anjadhe distributes only
long-public-domain historical figures.
