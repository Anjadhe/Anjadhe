# v0.1.0-alpha.26

Two days of work on the mail side of Anjadhe, and it shows. Email AI is now
an app of its own with a front page that says what needs you. The Inbox
learned which threads need a reply and which are waiting on someone else.
Routines and Automations became one thing, with an engine that fires when
mail arrives instead of on the next poll. And News grew a search box, a
bigger topic catalog, and an assistant that reads the article before it
answers.

## Added

- **Email AI is its own app.** What was a tab is now a full app (reachable
  from the app switcher and Cmd+K): insight folders on the left, that
  folder's rows in the middle, the open insight on the right. It opens on an
  **Overview** that leads with what is coming up (soonest first) and what
  arrived since you last looked, each row a door straight to its insight.
  The reading pane is a preference here too, folders never disappear just
  because they are empty, and the unread filter is the same envelope button
  the Inbox has.
- **Needs reply / Waiting on them.** Two computed lists at the top of the
  Inbox sidebar: mail a person sent you that you have not answered, and mail
  you sent that has been quiet for three days. Who wrote last is arithmetic;
  whether it is really a person expecting an answer is judged by your AI.
  Reply and it clears itself; archiving clears it too, and a right-click
  offers "No reply needed" / "Stop waiting on this" for the rest.
- **Right-click works on mail.** Inbox rows have a context menu: mark
  read/unread, archive, move to a bundle, and the thread-state exits above.
- **The AI reads attachments.** The assistant can open an attached PDF
  (scans included), spreadsheet, or document, and the insight engine can
  fill a bill's missing due date or amount from the attachment the mail
  points at. It fills blanks only; an attachment can never create an
  insight or make something actionable.
- **Routines that act, triggered by things.** Automations merged into
  Routines: one page for everything the app does on its own. A routine can
  run on a schedule or when an email or file arrives, as a read-only digest
  or a full task run that can act. Email triggers fire as mail lands, once
  per message; a routine's runs are listed on its own page with a
  per-run log; and a **Test trigger** button shows what would fire without
  running anything. Setting one up is a conversation: "Help me set up a
  routine" walks you through it.
- **Search in News.** A search box in the News header filters the current
  headlines (the last 48 hours of every topic you follow) and your Saved
  stories. Topics also carry 20 stories each now instead of 10.
- **A topic catalog you can search.** The topics dialog groups its
  suggestions (World, Tech & Science, Business, Sports, Culture & Life)
  with a filter box, and anything you type is a topic too. Up to 15 topics,
  and the limit is enforced out loud instead of silently dropping the ones
  past it.
- **Send Feedback.** Settings has a Send Feedback card that reaches the
  maker of Anjadhe directly, also available from the app menu and Cmd+K. It
  shows exactly what a send carries: your message, the app version and
  platform, and an app-details line you can untick. Attaching your
  analytics id so a report can be matched with usage data is a separate
  checkbox, off unless you check it, shown only if you opted into
  analytics.
- **A ticker's page links out.** A portfolio symbol's detail page opens the
  same symbol on the sites that trade or chart it.

## Changed

- **Insight folders say one thing each.** Bills, Receipts, Renewals,
  Appointments, Reservations, Deliveries, Deadlines, Codes, Security — one
  noun per folder, each stating its own definition, so where a mail lands
  is predictable. Marketing is not an insight, however much it talks about
  renewals or discounts. Existing insights were refiled automatically.
- **Your AI reads every incoming email now.** A keyword pre-filter used to
  decide which mail deserved analysis, and it missed real bills and
  receipts over phrasing. The model itself is the judge now. (Installs
  using a paid API key keep the thrifty pre-filter, because there each call
  is your money.)
- **An insight that became a task stays visible.** Email AI shows every
  insight from the last 90 days, including ones that turned into tasks; the
  task and the insight link to each other.
- **Opening a message counts as reading it sooner.** In the reading pane
  after one second instead of one and a half; opened full-width, instantly.
- **"Ask about this" in News reads before it answers.** The chat starts
  with the article's own text (not just the summary), and when web search
  is on, the assistant searches for background and newer developments
  instead of trusting what it remembers.
- **One market review.** The weekday pre-market, midday and close reviews
  collapsed into a single Market Review timed to your market's close,
  expressed in your own timezone.
- **Cmd+R asks before it kills AI work.** Reload used to silently end
  whatever the assistant, a routine, or a re-analysis run was doing. Now it
  says what is in flight and asks; work that can resume, resumes from any
  page.
- **A widget's color means the data's state** (amber warns, green is good,
  red alerts), never which app it belongs to.
- **Feedback tells the truth about your Mac.** Every Mac used to report
  itself as Intel; reports now carry the real chip and memory.
- **Choosing a remote brain frees the local model's RAM** instead of
  keeping the local engine loaded behind it.

## Fixed

- A custom news topic added past the fetch limit silently never loaded;
  every followed topic is fetched now.
- Websites that use bot protection (Zillow and many others) load in Browse
  again; sites that hard-block from the network get an honest banner with a
  way to open them in your default browser.
- An insight you delete stays deleted instead of returning on the next
  analysis pass.
- Following an insight into your mail is no longer a one-way door — Back
  returns to the page you came from, and the message no longer flashes and
  vanishes on the way in.
- Verification codes no longer become scheduled tasks that fire after the
  code is dead; they file under Codes with the code up front.
- API keys saved in Settings no longer read as corrupt after a relaunch
  (a startup-order bug in how the keychain was opened).
- An open Anjadhe window keeps the Mac from idle-sleeping mid-run, so
  long tasks finish.
- Cmd+K opens the palette even while writing a note; linking inside the
  editor moved to Cmd+Shift+K.
- A task due today can hop to tomorrow from its row menu.
- A routine digest that fails on a flaky connection retries quietly instead
  of posting the error to your feed, and a run interrupted by a restart
  picks itself back up.
