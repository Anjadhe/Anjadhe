# v0.1.0-alpha.48

Your notes and journal are now plain Markdown files you can open in Finder
or Obsidian, and the app says what it will cost.

## Added

- **Notes and Journal as files.** Every note is kept as a Markdown file in
  `~/Anjadhe/Notes/` and every journal entry in `~/Anjadhe/Journal/`
  (images beside them in `assets/`). Open them in Finder, any editor, or
  Obsidian: edits made to the files show up in Anjadhe, and edits in the
  app are written back. Renames and deletes travel both ways. The switch
  and an Open Folder button live in **Settings › Storage & Backup › Notes
  and Journal as Files** (on by default, per Mac). Sync between Macs is
  unchanged; the files are a mirror, not a new transport.
- **What Anjadhe will cost.** The Closed Alpha dialog in About (and the
  download page and FAQ on anjadhe.ai) now say it: free during alpha, a
  one-time license of about $79 with a year of updates and no account
  planned later, and everyone who installs during the alpha keeps the app
  free for good. Hosted extras keep a free monthly allowance.

## Changed

- **Contact the founder** at founder@anjadhe.ai from About, Send Feedback
  and What's new.
