# v0.1.0-alpha.70

## Added

- **Suggested prompts are written for you.** The "Try asking" pills on
  Home and in the assistant's empty state used to come from a fixed
  list. Your AI now writes six short prompts from what is actually going
  on: the apps you use, unfinished setup, task and project titles, the
  calendar, email insights, recent notes and chats. It refreshes about
  once an hour when something changed (less often on a metered cloud
  model), and only the data classes you allow to leave the Mac are ever
  in the sheet. The run shows in AI Activity.

## Changed

- **Colour as data.** Inbox, Email AI, Routines, News and Calendar each
  got a subtle colour pass: one hue per insight type, per sender, per
  routine and per followed topic, on tiles, chips and dots. Text,
  borders and cards stay ink.
- **Calendar's Sync button refreshes Apple and Google alike**, and it
  shows whenever either source exists.
- **Today is one list** on the Tasks page: timed rows first in clock
  order, a hairline, then the untimed ones. The "Anytime" card and its
  header are gone.
- **A web search that runs long now writes an answer.** The assistant
  has a per-turn search budget; past it, web_search says so and the
  turn writes from what was gathered instead of stopping on a canned
  message. A routine that hit the cap posts a disclosure, not "say
  continue".
- Public copy calls the product "personal AI" (README, About).

## Fixed

- **Home opens instantly again.** Returning to Home blocked the app for
  half a second or more on a store with many notes, because the feed
  re-read the whole notes blob fourteen times per paint. Reads are now
  cached in the app and the feed reads once. Measured on a real store:
  536 ms down to 18 ms.
- Apple Calendar events no longer blink out after every Google sync.
- The routine detail page threw before painting, so "Edit routine" and
  routine links landed on the list.
- The assistant's empty-state hero and "Try asking" block no longer
  re-render into a live conversation.
