# v0.1.0-alpha.107

A big one: nenva's new home, an assistant that judges instead of following
rules, and a Now page you can talk to.

## Added

- **Now walks you through what needs you, one card at a time.** Each card
  shows where it came from (email, a text, your calendar, tasks, a routine),
  holds still while you read it, and has **‹ ›** to move through the deck.
  The title opens its page; the main button does the obvious thing.
- **Talk to a card.** "Tell me what to do with this…" in any card opens that
  thing's own chat with the cursor ready and everything said about it
  before. One conversation per thing, wherever you reach it from, and the
  card shows its last exchange.
- **One item per real thing in your mail and texts.** Every reminder,
  change and confirmation about an appointment, a bill, an order, a booking
  or a subscription is kept together, with its one next step: a reply
  drafted for you to send, a payment page, a pickup. A payment confirmation
  closes the bill; a delivery closes the order. **Ignore** lets one go.
- **Trips.** Your flight, hotel, car, tables and tickets become one trip;
  nenva gathers the related messages (parking, insurance), notices nights
  with no hotel, and offers to help.
- **Work planned back from a due date.** For something due soon that needs
  time, nenva proposes work sessions in your free time; **Schedule N
  sessions** adds them, and a missed one offers a replan.
- **Today, with help on every row.** Each meeting and task gets one line of
  help and one action (Join, Prep 1:1, Mark done, Draft a reply…).
- **Check-ins and follow-ups chosen by your assistant**, from what is
  actually slipping, and only when worth asking.
- **Preferences by tap.** nenva asks a quick question when it matters
  (keep the market brief? use this reminder for new tasks? your usual work
  time?) and keeps your answer as a sentence in **Memory**.
- **Memory, rebuilt**: one list of short facts in your own words, kept up
  on every turn, with a clear note when something was not saved.
- **Jobs run on the team engine**, with each change asking you first in the
  chat, and a blue approval card when one waits.

## Changed

- **nenva's new shell**: a left nav (New chat · Now · Chats · Jobs ·
  Routines · Widgets · Settings), simpler Settings, a one-page first run,
  and mail that opens in Gmail.
- **Your assistant judges, rules don't.** What is worth telling you, what
  belongs together, which tools to use, what to remember and the order of
  your cards are the assistant's call, checked against the facts; your
  preferences are plain sentences in Memory. Only urgent things notify;
  the rest waits for the morning notice.
- Morning routines start around when your day starts, not at fixed times.
- The Portfolio AI switches moved into Memory as preferences.

## Fixed

- A reply that claims it changed something when nothing changed now says
  so ("Nothing was changed").
- Cards no longer change under you, and a card a chat already handled
  leaves Now.
- Focus mode, Pomodoro and the Setup Assistant are removed.
