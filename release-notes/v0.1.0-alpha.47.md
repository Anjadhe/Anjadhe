# v0.1.0-alpha.47

The assistant can now read the pictures in your email, Portfolio handles
after-hours quotes more quietly, and there is a proper support address.

## Added

- **The assistant reads email images.** On a model that can view images,
  asking about an email now includes its pictures: inline images pasted
  into the body and attached png, jpeg, webp or gif files. A bill that
  arrived as a screenshot, a flyer, or a receipt photo is read as part of
  the email instead of coming back empty. On a text-only model the
  assistant lists the images it could not read rather than guessing.
- **Portfolio accounts nav collapses.** The accounts sidebar has the same
  hamburger toggle Calendar has; the choice is remembered per Mac.

## Changed

- **After-hours quotes sit inline.** In Portfolio holdings the pre- and
  after-hours quote sits beside the price instead of on a second line, so
  rows keep one height during extended hours. The masthead's after-hours
  line adds a "value if this held" hint; the headline and totals stay
  regular-session.
- **Contact address is support@anjadhe.ai.** Every contact link in the
  app and on the website (About, Settings › Send Feedback, What's new, the
  site footer, privacy and terms pages) now goes to the support address.
