# v0.1.0-alpha.122

The chat rises over the page, reading your texts finds what needs you in
them, and a request in a chat is never mistaken for a note.

## Changed

- **The chat rises from the bottom.** A button on a Now card, a Today row,
  a card's "Tell me what to do with this…" box and the Commitments page all
  open the conversation as a sheet that rises over the page, beside the left
  nav, at the chat page's own size. Click the dimmed page above it, or
  press Escape, to close it.
- **Reading your texts is one switch.** On the Texts page, "Read my texts"
  now also lets your AI find what needs you in them; before, with nenva
  cloud, turning on only that switch read your texts and did nothing with
  them. If you turned texts on before, the page offers one tap to start.
- **Pinned opens Commitments.** Under your pins, a row opens the
  Commitments page, so you can find something to pin.
- On the Commitments page, your goals and projects are headed **Goals**
  (was "Moving").

## Fixed

- Asking for something in a commitment's chat ("add a pin to the home
  page") was sometimes kept as a note and answered "Noted", with nothing
  done. A request is now treated as a request, and a reply can no longer
  say something was done when nothing that ran did it.
- A note the assistant wrote opened blank on the Documents page.
- Settings › Memory: the list of facts no longer touches the box above it.
