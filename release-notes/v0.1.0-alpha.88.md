# v0.1.0-alpha.88

## Added

- **Everything a calendar event carries.** An event's details now show the
  organizer, who is coming and whether they said yes, the call link and
  dial-ins, reminders, attachments and more, for Google and Apple calendars
  alike. The home card's **Join** button and the assistant read the same
  details.
- **Current headlines from CNBC in Portfolio.** Turn it on under
  **Settings › Portfolio**: All Accounts and each ticker page get a short
  summary of what CNBC is running now, refreshed every 10 minutes while
  it is on screen. Off by default.
- **One switch for Portfolio's AI writing.** **Settings › Portfolio ›
  Generate stock profile using AI** turns the stock profiles and the
  daily brief off together. Off, neither is written or shown.

## Changed

- **The phone's Feed groups by routine.** One row per routine, new posts
  above ones you have read, previews without raw Markdown, and a reader
  that steps through the new posts. Failed runs can be opened too.
- **The Chrome extensions wear the new icon.**

## Fixed

- **Opening a routine from its result.** "The routine" on a home feed
  post opened the routine underneath the response. It now takes the page,
  and Back to the result returns you where you were.
