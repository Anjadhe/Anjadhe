# v0.1.0-alpha.24

This release is about the assistant finishing real work. It can now run
things while you are away, read your actual documents instead of guessing
from filenames, save a procedure that worked and replay it, and undo what
it changed. Settings got simpler, moving between apps got faster, and a
serious sync bug that could lose edits between Macs is fixed.

## Added

- **The assistant works while you are away.** Ask it to do something on a
  schedule ("every weekday at 7, check my portfolio against the news") or
  when something happens (a certain email arrives, a file changes) and it
  becomes an automation. It runs in the background, notifies you when it
  starts and finishes, and if it needs your OK partway through it waits
  and asks when you are back instead of guessing. The new **Automations**
  page shows everything that is armed, what ran, and what is waiting on
  you.
- **Permissions with limits.** A standing permission can now carry bounds:
  so many uses a day, an end date, and exceptions. The assistant can hold
  a standing permission to send email, say, and still ask before writing
  to someone brand-new. When a limit runs out it asks instead of acting;
  nothing is ever a silent yes. Review and revoke it all under **Standing
  permissions** on the Automations page.
- **It reads your documents.** PDFs (including scans, read on your Mac),
  Excel spreadsheets, Word documents, and images: the assistant now opens
  the file and reads what is inside, whether it found it in your folders
  or on a web page. "What did this statement charge me?" is answered from
  the statement, not the filename.
- **Recipes.** When a task finishes cleanly, you can save it as a recipe.
  Next time, the assistant replays the steps that worked instead of
  figuring everything out from scratch: faster, and far more predictable.
  Saved recipes are listed in Settings.
- **Undo for the assistant.** Everything the assistant changes is
  recorded. "Undo this turn" under a chat answer, or "Undo changes" on a
  finished task, puts things back. Anything you edited yourself in the
  meantime is left alone and reported rather than overwritten, and
  actions that cannot be taken back (a sent email) say so up front.
- **Your portfolio reviews itself.** On weekdays, three short notes
  appear in your home feed, written from your real positions and prices:
  a pre-market review around 7:30, a midday check around 12:30 that only
  speaks up when something is moving, and a market-close review around
  4:30 that says how the day went and whether you are still on plan.
  Edit the times or turn any of them off in Prompts.
- **Connections you can add in one click.** Settings now offers ready-made
  connections for the assistant: Browser, DeepWiki (ask about any public
  GitHub project), Context7 (current developer docs), and GitHub (your
  issues and pull requests, paste one token). Each one is tested the
  moment you add it, and problems are explained in plain words.
- **Ctrl+Tab switches between recent apps**, like browser tabs. Tap once
  to bounce back to the app you were just in; hold Ctrl and keep tapping
  to walk further back. A titlebar button opens the same strip with the
  mouse.
- **An app switcher in the titlebar.** The grid button beside the Anjadhe
  wordmark lists every app; click one to open it, or Customize to choose
  what shows where.
- **Light, Dark, or System theme** in Settings, with System following
  your Mac.
- **Answers link to what they made.** Files and folders the assistant
  wrote, prompts it scheduled, and records it touched appear as small
  buttons under the answer: click to open the record or reveal the file
  in Finder.
- **The terminal got friendlier.** Answers render as formatted text,
  /help, /model, /clear and /undo work as commands, and if sync is locked
  the terminal asks for your passphrase instead of failing.

## Changed

- **Settings is flatter and easier to find things in.** Each setting has
  its own page, every page fits one consistent width, and Cmd+K now takes
  you straight to a specific setting by name.
- **Portfolio is calmer.** Your accounts sit in a left nav; click one and
  the whole page scopes to it. Holdings are one simple row per position.
  The analysis moved into conversation with the assistant, which now has
  the full picture.

## Fixed

- **Edits are no longer lost between Macs.** With two Macs open, one
  could silently discard the other's work: portfolio transactions logged
  in the morning, note edits, prompt schedules. Portfolio, Notes, and
  prompt run-times now merge record by record, so both Macs keep what
  they did.
- **Scheduled prompts fire once, at their scheduled time.** A daily
  7:00 prompt could run three times in a day, or fire at odd hours,
  when two Macs disagreed about its last run. The assistant can also
  help with this now: ask it "why did my review run at 4pm?" and it can
  look at the prompt and fix the schedule.
- Reading a feed post now sticks across Macs, and post bodies use the
  full width of the column.
- Opening a note, task, or goal from Cmd+K opens that record, not just
  the app it lives in.
- Plan's "Other goals" section now shows only goals not filed under a
  focus area, instead of every goal. The same underlying bug inflated a
  few counts elsewhere, including the weekly review's completed count.
- Buttons on a detail page's suggestion chip no longer get cut off.
