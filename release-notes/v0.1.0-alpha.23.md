# v0.1.0-alpha.23

The home page and the way you move around the app both changed in this
release. The left sidebar is gone, Cmd+K is the launcher, and home now shows
what your apps are holding for you instead of a wall of icons.

## Added

- **A home page that shows what needs you**: under the message box, cards
  from the apps themselves — overdue tasks, what is due today, unread email
  insights, what is left on today's calendar, and your portfolio's value and
  movers. The rule is that a card with nothing to say does not appear at all,
  so home empties itself as you work and a calm day looks calm. Tasks can be
  ticked off straight from home.
- **Cmd+K is the launcher**: press it anywhere and you get your most-used
  apps first, then every other one. Keep typing and your own notes, tasks,
  goals, journal entries, bookmarks and focus areas join the results, ranked
  by how well they match. Arrows move, Enter opens, Esc closes.
- **A plan for your portfolio**: tell the assistant what the money is for,
  your horizon, the drop you could sit through, and the mix and limits you
  want — it interviews you one topic at a time rather than handing you a
  form. From then on Anjadhe measures your real holdings against the plan:
  which sleeves have drifted outside their bands, which guardrails are
  broken, what the plan does not cover. Those numbers are computed by the
  app, not written by the AI, and a trade that puts you off plan says so at
  the time.
- **Clear a whole bundle of mail**: drilling into a bundle now has Mark all
  read, Mark all unread and Archive all above the list, with a count of what
  they act on.
- **Escalating notices become one insight**: a courtesy notice, then an
  overdue notice, then a final notice is one thing to do, not three. The
  newest heads the insight and the earlier ones roll up into its timeline;
  nothing is lost, and "Not this" splits them back out. Overdue and
  return-by mail is also better caught in the first place.
- **A switch for after-hours prices** in Portfolio, so pre-market and
  post-market moves are yours to show or hide.

## Changed

- **The left sidebar is gone.** Every page gained that width back. Home is
  the wordmark in the middle of the titlebar, a Home button appears on the
  left whenever you are somewhere else, and the app list lives behind Cmd+K.
- **Home reads as a page**, not a stack of dashboard cards: a greeting and
  the date on one line, the message box under it, then your cards, then the
  feed — all on one left edge.
- **The feed is a digest, grouped by prompt.** Each background prompt is one
  block showing its newest result with a line or two of what it says, with
  older editions folded behind "Show N earlier". An Unread / All toggle
  switches between what still needs you and the whole history. Email
  insights are no longer mixed in; they have their own card above.
- **News is a page of its own**, and the news pane that used to sit on home
  is gone — one place to read news instead of two. The Morning News digest
  is now built from your actual topic headlines rather than a web search.
- **Profiles have been removed.** The Work/Personal switcher in the titlebar
  and its Settings page are gone, and everything you have is simply visible
  in each app. Anything that was filed under a second profile now shows up
  alongside the rest.
- The titlebar's controls are one size with one icon style, in black and
  white like the rest of the app.
- Journal drops its quick-capture box; search moved into the page header.
- Tasks: the task detail page drops **Duplicate**.
- Portfolio: **Import from file** is gone — attach the file in a chat and
  ask the assistant to add the transactions. With several accounts, the
  header now has an account picker rather than a row of tabs.
- Email bundle settings show what each bundle actually catches, so the
  description that teaches the classifier is visible while you edit it.
- Opening a message marks it read after a moment rather than instantly, so
  skimming under the Unread filter no longer empties the list under your
  cursor.

## Fixed

- **Browse: turning "Protect this site" off now actually turns it off.** The
  cookie and referrer protections ignored the switch, so sign-ins, checkouts
  and other cross-site flows kept failing on a page you had already
  unprotected.
- News: stories from Google News open the real article instead of a redirect
  page, and a story you revisit is no longer re-summarized from scratch.
- A reminder series no longer creates a duplicate task per reminder.
- AI-written text now renders with the same typography everywhere it appears
  — chat, feed posts, and news summaries.
