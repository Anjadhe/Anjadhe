# v0.1.0-alpha.77

## Added

- **Related coverage under a news summary.** When the reader finishes
  summarizing an article it runs one web search on the headline and lists a
  few real links to where the story continues — another outlet on the same
  story, background, what followed. Every title, link and publisher is the
  source's own; the AI only picks which of the results are worth your time,
  and the card says whether it did. A row opens in the reader; the arrow
  beside it opens the page in your browser. Needs web search on, and it
  shows nothing when it finds nothing.
- **A news source is also a scope.** With more than one source turned on,
  the News rail grows a Sources list: click Hacker News to read only its
  stories, on the front page, inside a topic and in search, and All sources
  to come back. The rail's counts follow the scope. It lasts for the
  session, not forever — it is a lens, not a setting.

## Changed

- **The demo deck reads on a phone.** The presentation on the site's home
  page now has a portrait stage rather than a letterboxed slide.
- **The assistant's help covers both News changes**, and the same articles
  on the website were updated with it.
