# v0.1.0-alpha.62

## Added

- **Documents.** Reader is now Documents: the place for your digital
  documents. Bring in PDFs, scans and photos of paper (read with the
  Mac's own text recognition), spreadsheets and CSV files, Word,
  PowerPoint and RTF files, web pages, text and Markdown. Everything is
  parsed and indexed on this Mac, and organized with tags instead of
  folders: a tag like "Finance/Taxes/2025" draws as a tree on the left,
  every row carries its tags, "+ tag" appears on hover, and "Rename tag"
  renames it everywhere. Dropping files on a tag imports them already
  tagged. The assistant can search within a tag, list your documents,
  and tag one for you. Settings › Documents holds the index and folder.
- **Cleaner reading.** The first time you open a document, the reader
  tidies the extracted text into readable Markdown, once, and keeps the
  result beside the file. This runs on its own when your AI model is on
  this Mac; otherwise a "Tidy with AI" button names where the text would
  go and the click is the consent. With a model that can see, PDFs and
  images are read from their rendered pages instead, and that
  transcription becomes the document's searchable text.
- **Sources under an answer.** When the assistant reads your documents,
  notes or journal to answer, the answer lists what it drew on as links
  back into the app.

## Changed

- **Writing voice is a setting.** The Writing Voices app is gone.
  Your own voice lives in Settings › Writing voice: turn it on, pick
  what it learns from (documents, Notes, Journal, sent emails), press
  Study, and edit the guide it writes. Drafting in it is something you
  ask the assistant for ("write this in my voice"). Custom voices built
  from other people's writing, the bundled starter voices and the
  routine "Voice" option were removed; routines write in the
  assistant's own voice.
- **Reading pane on laptops.** In Inbox, Email AI, Notes and Journal the
  reading pane now starts hidden on a laptop-sized display, so the list
  gets the full width and an item opens full width with a way back.
  The pane button turns it on, and the choice is remembered on this Mac.
- **Calendar's empty state is one line.** With no account connected the
  page shows a single connect hint above the grid instead of a full
  welcome; what the calendar can do moved into its "?" help article.
- **Fewer apps on by default.** A fresh install starts with Portfolio
  and News off in the launcher; More apps turns them on. Existing
  installs are unchanged.

## Fixed

- **Notes and Journal first-run welcome** was missing on a laptop
  display, where the hidden reading pane took the welcome with it. It
  now takes the full width until the first note or entry exists.
- **PDFs attached in chat** failed in the installed app with a
  "DOMMatrix is not defined" error, and a scanned PDF was refused as
  having no text. Both are fixed; scans are read with the Mac's own
  text recognition, as file reads already were.
