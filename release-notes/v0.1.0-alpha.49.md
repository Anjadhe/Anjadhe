# v0.1.0-alpha.49

Headlines about the stocks you hold, right where you look at them.

## Added

- **News on your holdings.** A ticker's page in Portfolio lists recent
  headlines about that company. Account pages and All Accounts collect
  headlines across the holdings (largest positions first; index funds are
  left out so companies are not crowded out). Headlines come from the same
  engine as the News app, cached per ticker on this Mac, fetched only when
  you open the page, and never written by a model. A headline opens the
  News reader (article fetched and summarized on your Mac) and its back
  button returns to Portfolio.
- **The assistant reads those headlines.** A new `get_holdings_news` tool
  answers "any news on my stocks?", "why is X moving?" and single-ticker
  questions with real, dated articles; the daily Market Review uses it too.
- **Smaller local models.** Qwen 3.5 9B (16 GB Macs) and 4B (8 GB Macs)
  are back in the catalog, so a local assistant is available on more
  machines. The low-RAM setup step now only covers Macs under 8 GB.

## Fixed

- **Reader mode kept losing the date.** The web browser's reader now reads
  the page's own metadata (JSON-LD, Open Graph, `<time>`) and shows author,
  site, publication date, last update, subtitle and reading time in a
  proper header, with the lead image when the text has none. It no longer
  repeats the headline or "By …" / "Published …" lines inside the article.
- Task mode no longer fails at intake when the goal names a file by path:
  a concrete path is treated as the material itself, and the read step is
  its existence check.
- **The assistant's help answers were broken in alpha.48.** A formatting slip
  in the built-in help text stopped it loading, so "how do I…" questions
  got no help topic. Fixed.
- Send Feedback's hint now says the message goes straight to the founder.
