# v0.1.0-alpha.22

## Added

- **Options in Portfolio**: track long calls and puts alongside stocks. The
  transaction editor has a Stock/Option toggle with underlying, call/put,
  strike and expiration; holdings and detail views show a readable label
  (AAPL $250 Call 12/18/26) with an expiry chip, and the 100x contract
  multiplier is applied to value, cash and P&L. Long positions only for now.
- **Account tabs in Portfolio**: an All Accounts tab plus one tab per account
  under the header, so accounts switch directly instead of going back through
  the dashboard. Holdings also cluster by symbol, so several lots of the same
  stock (or several contracts on the same underlying) fold into one row.
- **Email search that finds things**: words match in any order across sender,
  subject, snippet and locally synced message bodies, with one-letter typo
  tolerance, accent folding, "quoted phrases", and Gmail-style narrowing
  (`from:`, `to:`, `subject:`, `is:unread`).
- **A reading pane in Email**: a message opens beside the list instead of
  replacing it, with a header toggle for a full-width list and a breadcrumb
  that names where you are. Bundles drill in rather than expanding in place,
  and bundle cards read icon, name and date, then who has been writing lately.
- **Save a news story**: the article reader has a Save button, and the topics
  rail has a Saved list that keeps stories after the headline drops out of the
  feed. Your saved list syncs between your Macs.
- **Rails you can collapse**: News gains a topics rail and Calendar an
  accounts rail, both behind a hamburger beside the title and remembered per
  Mac. Picking a calendar account scopes the grid to just that account.
- **The assistant can reach your mail properly**: it searches with the same
  engine the app uses (bodies included), reports how far back each account is
  synced instead of presenting a partial window as full history, can fetch
  older mail from Gmail once you confirm the timeframe, and can extract
  structured data across many messages in one pass ("list every subscription
  charge this year").
- **After-hours prices** in Portfolio, from a batched quote that carries the
  pre-market and post-market moves.

## Changed

- **Email is a full-height workspace**: the page itself no longer scrolls, and
  the sidebar, list, message and insights panes each scroll on their own.
  Search and the Unread/All filter moved up into the header row, rows are one
  tight line, and unread reads as bolder ink instead of a shaded background.
  Starring is gone from the interface (Gmail stars still sync as data).
- **The mail list stays fast into the thousands of messages**: rows draw a page
  at a time and widen as you scroll.
- **One page geometry everywhere**: every page now uses the same content band,
  so the left rail stops sliding sideways when you enter or leave Email. Page
  titles all start at the same place, and the header buttons (help, settings,
  search, actions) sit in one order across apps.
- **Settings > AI Models**: calmer cards with a status dot and a Default chip,
  and "Add a model" opens straight into the local catalog sized to your Mac's
  RAM, with your own server, OpenAI and Anthropic behind "More options".
- The Labels and Accounts sidebar in Email collapses behind a hamburger.
- Portfolio's "Ask about this" buttons start a fresh conversation instead of
  adding to the last one.
- Anjadhe's opt-in analytics and the mobile-sync relay now run on Anjadhe
  Connect (api.anjadhe.com) rather than Cloudflare. Both stay keyless and
  carry no personal data.

## Fixed

- **Calendar put timed events on the wrong line**: an 8:30 meeting drew at
  8:00. Events now sit at their actual start minute, and titles are one clean
  line instead of being sliced through the middle.
- A damaged email search index could fail a mail write (marking a message
  unread) instead of just failing the search.
- Email: background sync no longer leaves "Load older emails" stuck on
  "Loading...".
- The assistant no longer mistakes a spread-out repeat for a stuck loop and
  ends a long research turn early.
- Web Browser: Home actually unloads the page, and audio from a page you
  navigated away from now stops.
- Portfolio: day change for shares bought today is measured from your purchase
  price instead of yesterday's close, so a morning dip you bought after is no
  longer shown as your loss.
- Email transaction extraction understands per-contract option pricing, and
  Create Transaction preselects the right portfolio account.
- The assistant's email tool returns the stored message body.
- Region-specific examples in the app's copy are now neutral or US-based.
