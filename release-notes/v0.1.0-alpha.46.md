# v0.1.0-alpha.46

Two small things people asked for: when a note was written, and a Portfolio
toggle that sometimes ignored you.

## Added

- **Created and edited dates in the note editor.** The line under a note's
  title now says when it was last edited and when it was created (hover
  for the exact times). Before, you had to guess from the list.

## Fixed

- **Portfolio "Show values" sometimes did nothing.** After visiting a
  ticker or property page and leaving Portfolio through the side nav or
  Back, the eye button repainted the page you had left instead of the one
  on screen, and only a relaunch fixed it. It now always repaints what
  you are looking at.
