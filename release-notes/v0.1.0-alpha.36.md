# v0.1.0-alpha.36

Trips assemble themselves from your reservations, records join the
conversation with @, and a whole goal plan can be rescheduled in one
step.

## Added

- **Trips.** Reservations that belong together — the flight out, the
  hotel, the rental car, the return leg — are grouped into a trip by
  their dates; nothing to set up. A coming trip gets its own card on
  Home, and the **Trips** entry in Email AI's sidebar opens the index:
  upcoming trips soonest first, past trips below. Opening a trip shows
  every reservation in one place, including bookings made months ahead
  whose email has long since aged out of the 90-day insight window.
- **Mention a record with @.** Type @ in any assistant message box to
  pull up your tasks, goals, notes, routines, strategies and accounts;
  the first word can narrow the type ("@goal marathon"). Picking one
  attaches the conversation to that record — current state and its
  decisions included — with a banner naming the attachment and an ✕
  to detach.
- **Reschedule a whole plan at once.** "Start this goal today" now
  shifts every task in the goal's plan in a single step — spacing
  preserved, weekday cadence optionally kept — with the full
  before/after preview in the confirmation dialog. No more one-task-
  at-a-time edits.
- **Paste images into notes.** An image or screenshot pastes straight
  into the note body, is stored inside the note (scaled down to keep
  notes light), and syncs with it.
- **Skipped insights are inspectable.** Everything Email AI learned to
  skip is listed under Email Settings › Skipped insights, each with a
  "Show again" undo; right-click a message in the inbox and choose
  "Analyze with AI again" to re-run it after unlearning.
- **Study depth.** Settings › Reader & Writing Voices sets how much of
  a voice's documents one Study pass reads.

## Changed

- **Reader search runs on a real vector index** — semantic search over
  a large library is much faster, with the same results.
- **Journal panes collapse** like Notes' now, and both Notes and
  Journal remember your reading-pane preference per Mac.
- **Email AI rows read like the Inbox's**: the whole row responds to
  hover and carries the same hover "Mark read" pill.
- **Goals' left nav drops completed goals**, so the group list is what
  is still in play.

## Fixed

- **HTML-only email is finally read in full.** Messages with no plain-
  text part (a large share of commercial mail) were being analyzed
  from their one-line preview snippet, which is why some obvious
  insights came out wrong or thin. The full message text is now
  derived from the HTML for analysis, matching, and chat over the
  open message. A purchased booking also files as a reservation now,
  not a receipt, so it joins its trip.
- **Long emails and long notes no longer get cut off mid-read** when
  the assistant works with them — it pages through the rest instead.
