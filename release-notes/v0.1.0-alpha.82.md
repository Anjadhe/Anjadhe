# v0.1.0-alpha.82

## Added

- **Routines can say nothing.** A scheduled run that finds nothing worth
  telling you now posts nothing at all, instead of a card that says so. It
  still counts as a run that worked: the routine's page marks it quiet, and
  five quiet runs in a row say so out loud and point at the prompt.
- **Try a routine before you arm it.** A new routine has no **Create
  routine** button until you have run its prompt once in front of you: a
  real run for one that writes an answer, a planned run that executes
  nothing for one that acts. A preview posts nothing, schedules nothing and
  creates nothing. Under the result, **What should be different?** rewrites
  the instruction, with Undo. Any edit takes the button away until you try
  the new version.
- **Repeat this.** A button beside Copy on any assistant answer turns that
  answer into a routine: it names it, suggests how often, and asks you only
  when it should run. It is never offered in a private chat.
- **Which model answers what.** With two or more models set up, **Settings
  › AI Assistant › Models** gains a section that sends one part of the app
  to a different model: Email and texts, News, Documents, Portfolio,
  Routines and tasks, or Small jobs (memory, filing a task, suggested
  prompts). Chat runs on whichever model is marked **Default**, and every
  row left on **Follows the default model** moves with that choice. This
  Mac runs one local model at a time, so a second local model is offered
  only in place of the first; work sent to a model off this Mac runs beside
  your chat instead of queueing behind it. Cloud Privacy still decides what
  may leave, asked about the model that part runs on.
- **Change a routine by talking to it.** The routine's page carries quoted
  asks under its prompt: make it weekly, make it shorter, let it search the
  web, why does it never find anything, or change what it does. The
  approval dialog shows the edit as "from" and "to" for the fields that
  actually change, so you see it before you say yes, and Undo takes it back.
- **A routine can be about something.** A routine can point at a record: a
  project, a ticker, an account, a document. Its page shows an **About** row
  that opens that record, the run carries the record's own context, and
  renaming the record renames what the routine says about it.
- **Your routines' day, in one line.** Home opens with what your routines
  did today, one row per routine: its name, and the headline of what it
  said, each a door to that edition. A run that stayed quiet reads "nothing
  to report".
- **A routine nobody reads offers to go.** Each routine shows how often its
  posts get opened ("opened 1 of 6"). After five editions with one or none
  opened, it offers to become weekly, to be deleted, or to be kept; keeping
  is remembered on this Mac and never asked again. A routine in a quiet
  stretch is never asked.
- **Say when a routine runs, in a sentence.** The form's **Runs when** takes
  plain language ("every weekday at 7am", "when an email from billing@
  mentions an invoice") and fills in the fields with the sentence said back
  to you. Anything it cannot express it names, with the closest thing it can
  do, rather than quietly running something else. The pickers stay as "Or
  pick it".
- **Examples on an empty Routines page.** Three starting points to open in
  the assistant: what is due in the next ten days, prep me for today's
  meetings, and turn school or billing email into tasks.

## Changed

- **Send Feedback opens the founder page** rather than a blank mail window,
  so you can see who you are writing to and the other ways to reach him. The
  direct address stays in About, What's new and the license card. The
  diagnostics the card shows are now one labelled fact per line.
- **The app asks once whether you will share anonymous usage,** with the
  switch that turns it off again named in the ask. It stays off unless you
  say yes.
- **Routine list furniture appears only when the list is long.** Sort
  headers and the interval filter show up at twenty routines; search is
  there at every size.
- **Editing a routine from its result opens the routine over the post it
  wrote,** with "Back to the result", instead of jumping to another page.

## Fixed

- **The day's line reads as sentences, not fragments.** It no longer clips
  clauses into ellipses: a routine's headline is said whole or left out, a
  lead-in before a colon is not mistaken for a headline, and a dated title
  heading is skipped rather than becoming the headline. The block also has
  its own padding again, instead of sitting flush against the card edges.
