# v0.1.0-alpha.80

## Fixed

- **Routines no longer lose what you typed.** Writing a new routine (or
  editing one), going to another page and coming back used to show an
  empty form. The form now keeps a draft on this Mac as you type and
  reopens with it, even after a restart. The Routines list and the
  routine's page say when a draft is waiting, with **Continue** and
  **Discard**. Nothing is created or scheduled until you click **Create
  routine**; **Cancel** throws the draft away, with Undo.
