# v0.1.0-alpha.111

Settings is one page all the way down, and Finance and Documents are
calmer, in the language of Now and Settings.

## Added

- **The article reader is back for Finance.** A headline on a money page
  (holdings news, CNBC's list) opens in nenva: the article is read on this
  Mac and summarized by your model from that text alone (from search
  coverage when the page will not load, or the article's own words with
  no model; each summary says which), with related coverage, the source
  block from the page's own metadata, Open article and Ask about it.
- **Settings › Advanced** lists only what the one page does not cover:
  Name, Model options, Web Search, Tool Servers, Permissions, AI Activity
  and the logs, Telegram and iMessage, Paired Devices, Locked apps,
  Storage, More apps, Documents, Finance, Developer.

## Changed

- **Settings is one page.** Backup, Lock, What left this Mac (date,
  service and kind from the ledger, never a prompt) and About are pages
  of the one Settings; every door in the app (the chat box, Insights,
  Now, the menu bar, About) lands on it. The Developer options switch is
  gone: nothing in Settings is behind a mode. The per-class Cloud Privacy
  matrix, the "Which model answers what" table, the analytics inspector
  and the Writing voice page left with it (the voice itself, and "write
  this in my voice", stay).
- **Finance is calm.** One centred column, no left nav: All Accounts
  lists the places as rows, the breadcrumb is the way back, and the
  header keeps the Show/Hide eye and a ⋯ menu for everything else. The
  masthead is a lede, one number
  and two plain lines; Today is one section (brief, CNBC's summary,
  holdings news); tables carry five columns with only Today coloured;
  the ticker page reads in order with one chart (Price | Your value).
  Property, loan, cash, Strategy and Spending pages wear the same shape.
- **Written sections fold while they are written.** The ticker profile,
  the daily brief and CNBC's summary show only "Writing with <model>…"
  until the text lands; no partial, no old text.
- **Documents is calm.** One column; the left tag tree is one quiet row
  of tags, one level at a time, with counts and still a drop target; a
  breadcrumb names where you are and is the way back; Import beside a ⋯
  menu (Open the folder, New tag, Rename this tag, settings, Help); the
  reader is a masthead over prose at reading width. The AI tidy shows
  only when whole.
- **Commitments:** Ignore, Add a note and Delete sit in plain view under
  the sheet; the More fold is gone. **Someday is retired**: anything
  parked there is open again with the day and repeat it held.
- The in-app help names the new Settings paths.
