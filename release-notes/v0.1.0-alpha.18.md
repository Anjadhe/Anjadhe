# v0.1.0-alpha.18

## Added

- **Wellness, a new built-in app**: one place to log everything about your
  health. Blood pressure and pulse, resting heart rate, blood glucose, SpO2,
  temperature, weight, activities with heart-rate data, steps, meals, water
  (with a one-click quick add), sleep, mood and energy, medications, and
  symptoms. Blood pressure and glucose readings show how long after your last
  meal and activity they were taken, so the number carries its context. Trend
  charts cover every metric with 2-week to all-time ranges; a Units button
  switches lb/kg, mg/dL/mmol/L, F/C, and oz/ml. If you built the earlier
  blood-pressure tracker as a custom app, Wellness offers a one-time import
  of all its data. The assistant can log entries ("log my bp, 122 over 79"),
  list them, and summarize trends.
- **Discover pane (preview, off by default)**: an optional right-hand pane on
  the home page with fresh links for topics you pick. Turn it on in
  Settings, under Appearance; it also needs web search enabled. Off unless
  you enable it, on new and existing installs alike.
- **Wide monitors use their width**: on large windows the home page arranges
  the rail, feed, and Discover side by side instead of one narrow column.
- **Feed posts from background prompts have read/unread state**, and one
  "Hide read items" preference now covers the whole feed.

## Changed

- **Email opens inline**: clicking a message keeps the mailbox sidebar in
  place instead of taking over the whole page, and the viewer's action
  buttons are quieter ghost chips.
- **Help no longer opens itself** the first time you visit an app.

## Fixed

- **Background email refresh no longer resurrects the email view** over
  whatever app you were using.
