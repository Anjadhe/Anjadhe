# v0.1.0-alpha.81

## Added

- **Portfolio › Tickers has a second view: Indicators.** The same tickers
  you already see, wearing the one-word verdicts from each one's daily
  business profile — valuation, how sound the business is, where AI fits,
  near- and long-term risk and value, the tone of the news — with a
  sortable column each. Click **Valuation** to bring the cheap ones to the
  top, **Near risk** for the quietest, **Long value** for the strongest
  case; the first click on any column sorts best-first. Every word is your
  AI model's own judgment from that day's profile, never a number the app
  computed, and there is no overall score. A ticker with no profile yet
  reads "—" and sinks to the bottom rather than pretending to be a low
  score. A row opens the ticker, where the reasoning behind the word is.

## Changed

- **Ticker rows lost their letter tiles.** The little coloured square in
  front of each symbol spelled out the symbol that was already there.
  Holdings, transactions, the watchlist, the cash row and both Tickers
  views now lead with the symbol itself.
- **One back button.** The back out of an email, an insight and a news
  article is now the same control everywhere — the Inbox's had quietly
  picked up a different font and read heavier than the others.
