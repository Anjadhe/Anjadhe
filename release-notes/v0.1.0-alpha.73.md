# v0.1.0-alpha.73

## Added

- **The assistant can send texts.** Ask it to "text Priya that I'm running
  late" or "send myself a reminder by iMessage" and it sends through the
  Messages app on this Mac, once either iMessage card in **Settings ›
  Accounts** is set up. It shows the recipient and the exact wording first;
  a text to another person asks for your OK every time, and a standing
  permission can only ever cover texts to your own number. It knows your
  number from the Notifications card and a person's name from the
  conversations Insights from your texts has read; otherwise give it the
  number.

## Changed

- **"Send a message" no longer means email by default.** When texts are set
  up on this Mac and you say "send a message" without naming a channel, the
  assistant asks whether you mean a text or an email. A phone number or the
  word "text" means a text; "email" or an address means email. Previously
  a message with no email word in it also shipped no email tools at all,
  so the assistant claimed it could not send mail.
- **Insights from your texts reads the last 30 days on the first run**
  (was 3). Existing installs that already turned it on get the wider window
  by turning the switch off and on again; tasks stay.

## Fixed

- **Full Disk Access is a step you take by hand, and the app now says so.**
  macOS never lists Anjadhe under Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access on
  its own, so "turn on Anjadhe" pointed at a switch that was not there.
  The card, the error message, the help article and What's new now say:
  click the + button, choose Anjadhe from Applications, then quit and
  reopen the app. The card also explains why the first count is smaller
  than your Messages list (texts are grouped by conversation).
