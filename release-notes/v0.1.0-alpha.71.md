# v0.1.0-alpha.71

## Changed

- **Documents, redesigned.** The list is one card with a composition bar
  showing what kinds of files a tag holds, rows lead with a file-type tile
  over a size, date and passage count line, the tag tree carries a colour
  dot per tag family (children wear their parent's colour), search results
  are one card per document, and the reader opens with an eyebrow, a
  display title, pill buttons and the AI tidy banner as a card. Tag chips,
  the composition bar and the AI banner carry colour; icons stay ink.
- **Icons are monochrome again in News.** The publisher tile, the
  Catch-me-up sparkle, the event date tiles and the "For you" mark are ink
  on the page colour. Topic colours stay on the dots and chips.
- **The first-run setup is a sheet.** Each step sits in one lifted card
  with its buttons in a footer, the step indicator above it, and a larger
  welcome. No copy changed.
- **Terminal access is now an experimental feature, off by default.** The
  `anjadhe` command, its Settings › AI Assistant › Terminal Access page and
  the local server it needs only exist once you turn on **Terminal access**
  under Settings › Developer › Experimental features. If you had it on
  before, it stays off until you flip that switch; your earlier setting is
  kept and comes back with it. Nothing listens while the switch is off.
