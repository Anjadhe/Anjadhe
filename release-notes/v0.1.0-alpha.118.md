# v0.1.0-alpha.118

nenva on your phone through Telegram, set up in three steps, and paid plans
for the United States.

## Added

- **On your phone.** A new row on Settings sets up Telegram in three
  guided steps: make your bot with BotFather, paste its token, and press
  Start in Telegram to link your phone. Two switches: you reach nenva, and
  nenva reaches you.
- **nenva reaches you only for what matters.** On Telegram, nenva writes to
  you about a card that is new on Now, a commitment you gave a time, and
  anything waiting for your approval, with Allow and Not now buttons you
  can answer from the phone or the Mac. Other notifications stay on the Mac.

## Changed

- **Paid plans are for the United States for now.** Settings › Plan offers
  Plus, Pro and top-ups on a Mac set to the US region; elsewhere it says
  so. A plan code bought in the US works on any Mac.
