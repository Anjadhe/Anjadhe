# v0.1.0-alpha.108

Tasks and projects become one list the assistant keeps, a money coach, and
one folder for each real thing in your life.

## Added

- **Commitments.** Tasks and Projects are rebuilt as one list of what you
  said you would do; a big one holds smaller ones. Say it in plain words and
  the assistant proposes the dates and changes, and you tap to accept. Every
  change has Undo. Your existing tasks and projects are carried over, and
  the old pages keep working. Its door is a row on **Settings › Memory**.
- **Tell it how it went.** What you say about one day in a commitment's
  chat is filed on that day, and nenva asks after the ones worth asking
  about, a couple of hours after they end.
- **A money coach.** nenva looks across your accounts, bills, subscriptions,
  receipts in your mail and your money goals, and raises a card on Now when
  something is worth a look. Ask about your money in any chat, or set a
  goal by talking. It never predicts prices, and nothing is paid, moved,
  bought or sold from here.
- **A bank payment closes the bill it paid**, when your accounts are linked.
- **Your own tasks and calendar events join their folder.** The office's
  email, its text, the calendar event and your task about one appointment
  are one item. A chat about an item writes what you decide into it.
- **Connectors**: one searchable list in Settings for Gmail, Google
  Calendar, Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders.
- **A box on the page.** The Commitments page and each commitment have an
  assistant box; a question opens that thing's chat over the page.

## Changed

- **One folder per real thing.** Before making a new folder nenva looks
  more widely for one that already exists, and two folders for the same
  thing are joined, with Undo. You can ask it to join, split or move a
  message out.
- **Connecting Gmail reads the last two weeks** (up to 300 messages)
  instead of the 50 newest, and only mail from the last 48 hours can alert
  you right away.
- **Now holds still while it reads.** Cards from a first read arrive
  together when the reading is done; the status line shows what is being
  read with a small turning ring, and **Look again** answers with what it
  found.
- **No routines until you ask.** A new install arms no starter routines.
  Routines already on your Mac are yours and are left alone.
- **The main button on each page is blue.**
- **The Spending app is hidden for now** (Settings › Developer ›
  Experimental features brings it back). Its data stays on your Mac.
- Old open Apple reminders (untouched for 90 days) come in under Someday
  instead of as overdue tasks.
- Google Calendar is checked from the moment the app starts, so Now sees a
  new event without opening Calendar.
- On the iPhone, News loads on the phone first, so headlines are fresher.
- "Ignore these" said in a card's chat is remembered for that sender, not
  only for the one card.

## Fixed

- A chat continued on a later day no longer reads an old "tomorrow" as
  today's tomorrow; a commitment's chat always knows where it stands now.
- A reply is checked against what actually ran before it is shown, so it
  cannot say something was done or scheduled when it was not.
- Answering a check-in closes it, and a plan that is over is not asked
  about.
- On Now, a card with a short title no longer shrinks to the width of its
  text.
