# v0.1.0-alpha.121

Routines tell you what changed, nenva coaches your goals, and Finance keeps
to your own picture.

## Added

- **Routines that tell you only what changed.** A routine that keeps track
  of something now starts from what it already told you and the notes it
  keeps, stays quiet while nothing moves, and when something does, says
  exactly what changed, before and after ("EB-2 India Final Action:
  01NOV13 → 01JAN14"), on one card on Now. A routine that brings something
  every time (a roundup) covers only what is new since the last one.
- **One sentence, one tap.** Setting up a routine ends with nenva saying it
  back in one sentence, with its own reading of the schedule ("Every
  weekday at 7:00 AM, I'll bring you the quantum computing news you have
  not seen."). It also decides where the request belongs: something you do
  at a time becomes a commitment, a morning overview of your day is your
  Now page, a routine about your mail becomes an instruction the mail
  reader follows as messages arrive, and one about your money an
  instruction the money coach follows.
- **Your existing routines, converted with a tap.** Each routine made
  before this shows a card on Now with how it would work from now on. Yes
  changes it (your routines' settings are backed up first, and Undo puts
  one back); Keep it as it is leaves it alone. Nothing changes without your
  yes, and every routine keeps its time.
- **A coach for your goals.** nenva looks at your fitness, health,
  parenting and learning goals the way it looks at your money: where each
  stands, the stage running now, what you said on its days. It raises a
  card on Now when a stage is ending without a next one, sessions are
  slipping or a milestone was reached, and looks back when a stage ends.
  A goal shows its progress as a chart and one sentence of arithmetic, and
  a figure arrives by saying it in the goal's chat. Goals made before are
  offered to the coach with a card. Health readings leave this Mac only if
  you say yes once.
- **Pinned.** Pin a commitment (a project, a habit, a training plan) and it
  sits on the new Pinned page in the left nav, one tap from its own page.
- **Finance pages have their own chat.** The overview, each account, each
  holding and your plan each have one conversation beside the page; a
  standing fact you say there is kept on the record.

## Changed

- **Failed routine runs stay quiet.** One failed run no longer puts a
  "didn't finish" card on Now. A routine that keeps failing raises one
  card, and when the model is the cause (unreachable, or the cloud
  allowance is used up) there is one card for all of them.
- **Finance keeps to your own picture**: accounts, holdings and their
  history, transactions, properties, debts, net worth, your plan, live
  prices and Spending. News on holdings, CNBC headlines, the daily AI
  brief, AI ticker profiles, the Tickers page, the Watchlist and account
  sharing are gone; anything you saved stays in your data.
- A money goal's progress comes from the accounts that count toward it.
- "Add a step" on a commitment saves the step as soon as you press Enter,
  with Undo.
- A text to someone without iMessage now goes out as SMS, and nenva says
  whether it was delivered instead of assuming it was.
- The morning briefing is no longer suggested as a routine: that is what
  Now is.
- The menu bar's View updates and the old Updates page it opened are gone.

## Fixed

- A daily routine no longer repeats the same unchanged result every day.
