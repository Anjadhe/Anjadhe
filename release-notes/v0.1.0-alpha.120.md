# v0.1.0-alpha.120

Anything you can do in nenva, you can now ask nenva to do.

## Added

- **Ask instead of tapping.** From any chat, the assistant can do what the
  buttons do: set a card on Now aside, mark a folder's step done or ignore
  it, mute a sender, say a message was not useful, answer a suggested work
  plan, run or stop a routine, pause all scheduled routines, stop or resume
  a job, back up now, move a document to the Trash, add a file from your
  Mac to Documents, find or delete an old chat, star a memory or confirm it
  still holds, and switch between light and dark. Anything that removes
  something or changes what happens later asks you first.
- **Undo by asking.** Say "undo that" and the assistant lists what can be
  put back, that chat's earlier changes and the latest changes to your
  commitments, and asks before undoing one.
- **Fix Finance entries in a chat.** Correct or delete a buy or sell (cash
  moves as it does on the page), rename an account or set its type, remove
  a paid-off loan or a sold home, and keep a watchlist.
- **It can see what you see.** The assistant reads your Now page and what
  Settings shows: which sources are on and whether the AI may read them,
  this month's nenva cloud use, your last backup, Lock, Telegram and paired
  phones. Ask "are you reading my texts?" or "when did I last back up?".

## Changed

- **Preferences said in a chat now take effect.** Saying "stop writing the
  market brief" changes what nenva does, not only the sentence in Memory.
- A task the assistant adds from an email keeps its link to that email.
- Jobs started by a routine show the routine's name.
- Writing voices are fully gone; anything you saved with them stays in your
  data.
