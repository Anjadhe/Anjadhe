# v0.1.0-alpha.109

A repeating commitment can be moved for one day only, and the assistant
keeps a plan for later apart from a report of what happened.

## Added

- **One day at another time.** "I'll do the workout this evening, just for
  today" moves that day's occurrence only; the usual time of the repeat is
  untouched. Today, reminders, the calendar and the phone show the moved
  time while it is that day, and a day moved after its morning reminder
  rings again. The assistant asks before it moves the day.

## Changed

- **A plan is not a report.** "Not done yet, I'll do it later today" is
  kept on the day as a plan, marks nothing, and nenva waits for the evening
  before asking how it went instead of asking in the afternoon.
- **One owner for when you do it.** A plan said in a commitment's own chat
  belongs to that commitment: it is not stored as a memory fact and does not
  become a separate check-in.
- Changing a commitment's time with the usual tools now says plainly that it
  changes every occurrence.

## Fixed

- A one-day change no longer turns into the repeat's new usual time (every
  Monday had become 6:30 PM after one evening workout).
- A reply naming a clock for a day is checked against what the record holds
  for that day, even after a change succeeded.
- The day's one check-in look ran every hour because its "already judged"
  mark was lost on each read; it is kept now.
