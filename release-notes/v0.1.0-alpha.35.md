# v0.1.0-alpha.35

The Reader arrives and Writing Voices turns on for everyone, Wellness
grows from a health log into a coach, and Notes gets collapsible panes.

## Added

- **Reader** — a new app for your imported documents (reports, essays,
  papers, statements). Import files, drag-drop, or drop them into the
  folder in Finder; search everything by meaning, open a document in
  place with matches marked, and **Ask about this document** starts a
  chat that stays attached to it — the assistant answers from the
  document's actual text, never from memory of a familiar title.
- **Writing Voices is on for everyone** — no more experimental flag. Two
  sample voices are bundled (Mark Twain and Abraham Lincoln, from their
  public-domain works) and import with one click, so you can hear what a
  studied voice sounds like before teaching your own.
- **Routines can speak in a voice.** A digest routine — a daily review,
  a morning motivator — can carry a Writing Voice for its posts. Pick it
  on the routine's form; the assistant's own voice stays the default.
- **Wellness is a coach now.** The page opens with a quick-log line —
  type "BP 128/82 after my walk" or "strength training 45 min" and the
  assistant logs it with every detail kept — over a Today strip and an
  attention briefing (streaks, trends, what you usually log and
  haven't) computed from your entries, never guessed. One click arms a
  weekly honest review or a daily motivator, optionally in a voice you
  taught. Habit-level only; it never diagnoses.
- **The assistant can correct the log.** Fix a mistyped reading or
  delete an entry by asking — corrections update the entry in place,
  and deletes ask first.
- **The assistant can delete notes** (with a confirmation, like every
  delete).
- **Search in the AI logs.** Settings › the model and search logs now
  filter as you type — who called, which model, what was sent, what
  came back.

## Changed

- **Writing Voices slims to its name**: the voices list and each
  voice's documents. Global search, other documents, and the corpus
  reader moved to Reader — the two apps are lenses over one folder,
  and every document is visible in both.
- **Notes panes collapse individually.** The filters column and the
  note list each get their own toggle, remembered per Mac; opening a
  note from elsewhere (a goal, a record link) folds them just for that
  visit.

## Fixed

- **Clicking between paragraphs in a note no longer adds empty
  lines.** The editor only inserts a paragraph where the caret truly
  had nowhere to land (between two code blocks, images, or tables).
- **"Summarize this document" over an open document now works even
  when phrased loosely** — the chat carries the document itself, so
  the assistant no longer answers "I don't have a tool for that" or
  summarizes the famous book instead of your copy.
