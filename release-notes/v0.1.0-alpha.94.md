# v0.1.0-alpha.94

## Changed

- **The app is called Anjadhe again.** The name nenva now belongs to a
  separate, new product; this app goes back to the name it started with.
  Everything you read says Anjadhe: the Dock and menu bar, the app icon
  (a serif A), Help, notifications, Anjadhe Cloud and the browser
  extensions. Nothing about your data, your accounts or your settings
  changes.
- **Your files are in ~/Anjadhe again.** On first launch the folder that
  holds your notes, journal, documents and apps moves back from ~/nenva to
  ~/Anjadhe, in one step on the same disk. A ~/nenva link is left behind,
  so anything that remembered the old path, like an Obsidian vault, keeps
  working.
