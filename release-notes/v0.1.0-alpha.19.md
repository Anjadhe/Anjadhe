# v0.1.0-alpha.19

## Added

- **Name your assistant**: first-run setup now ends with an optional step to
  give the assistant a name, and existing installs get a small, dismissible
  "Your assistant can have a name" line on the home page and in the assistant
  view. You can also set, change, or remove the name anytime from the Name
  field at the top of Settings, under AI. The name replaces the "AI Assistant"
  label everywhere it appears — the sidebar, the chat header, and the "Ask
  Anjadhe anything" box — and the assistant knows its name and answers to it.
  It syncs to your other Macs. Skipping is remembered, so you're only asked
  once; leave the field blank to keep the generic label.
