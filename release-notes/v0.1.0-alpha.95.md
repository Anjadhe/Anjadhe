# v0.1.0-alpha.95

## Fixed

- **Starting a new chat no longer deletes your recent conversations.** Once
  you had more than 50 conversations, starting a new one trimmed the list
  by position instead of by date, and the trimmed conversations were
  deleted on every device. The ones most often cut were the newest,
  including chats from your iPhone. Now the least recently used ones are
  only set aside on this Mac, and nothing is deleted. Conversations lost
  this way can be restored from an iCloud backup.
