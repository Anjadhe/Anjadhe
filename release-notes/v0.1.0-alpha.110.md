# v0.1.0-alpha.110

nenva folds to three pages — Now, Chats, Settings — around one job:
keeping a household's bills, appointments, renewals and replies from
slipping. A thing you track is a commitment, a thing you know is memory,
a thing you have is a source; nothing else earns a page.

## Added

- **Coming up.** Now shows the next seven days under Today: your
  commitments and your meetings, joined from the records.
- **A card on Now is a record.** One card per thing, settled by the record
  or by you, and it comes back only with a stated reason. What you say in
  a thing's chat ("just ignore it", "paid it yesterday") reaches its card
  and its folder before the reply, with a receipt and Undo.
- **Sources, one list.** Settings › Connectors lists every outside place
  nenva reads — Gmail, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Apple Reminders,
  Apple Notes, Texts, Files — each with a small page: a switch, what it
  reads, when it last did. Settings › Privacy's "what nenva reads" lines
  are built from the same list.
- **Apple Reminders works both ways.** Marking a reminder done, changing
  its title or day, or deleting it in nenva writes back to Apple
  Reminders; Undo writes back too.
- **Apple Notes as an index.** The assistant can search and read your
  Apple Notes, naming the source; nothing is copied into nenva.
- **Settings is one page.** Model, Memory (with Commitments), Connectors,
  Privacy in plain sentences, Backup, Appearance, About, and a Developer
  options switch behind which "All settings" opens the full settings.

## Changed

- **Chats holds the jobs and the routines.** A job's status rides its
  chat's row and the job opens from the chat; a routine is a Standing chat
  on the Chats page, Edit opens its chat, and setting one up is a
  conversation that tries the routine before you approve it. The Jobs,
  Routines and Widgets pages are gone from the nav.
- **Tasks, Projects and Calendar pages are gone.** A task is a commitment,
  a project is a big commitment with smaller ones under it (Settings ›
  Memory › Commitments), and your calendars are read onto Now. An event
  opens where it lives. Insights opens from a card.
- **Notes is Text Documents**: the canvas for what the assistant writes
  for you, and anything you write yourself. Every note you had is still
  there, and the Markdown files now live in `~/nenva/Documents`;
  `~/nenva/Notes` and `~/nenva/Journal` are left as they are.
- **Finance** is one app: Investments and Spending, linked through its
  nav. Wellness readings are commitments now ("Track blood pressure",
  "Take <medication>"), filed from chat without approval.
- **The Journal app is removed.** Nothing was deleted: every entry stays
  in nenva's data, and one final Markdown export of all of them was
  written to `~/nenva/Journal` (a one-time notice with Show folder).
- **Removed with the fold:** News and the Chrome reader extension,
  Bookmarks, the Noticing and Open threads previews, terminal access (the
  `anjadhe` CLI), Mac control, the Workrooms page (every job still runs on
  its engine), and the full shell with its side nav and app switcher.
  Stored data of removed features is left in place, unread.
- The in-app help answers from the new shape of the app: Commitments,
  Connectors, the one-page Settings, where the Journal went.

## Fixed

- A chat about a task resolves its record again, and the Commitments
  sheet is the record the assistant sees in front of it.
- Show folder on the Journal export notice opens the folder.
- Two text documents whose titles differ only in case get their own files.
