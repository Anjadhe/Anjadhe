# v0.1.0-alpha.60

## Changed

- **Settings › Accounts, redrawn.** Google accounts, the Apple apps,
  Telegram and iMessage now share one card layout: a title over a single
  status line with a green, amber or red dot, the switch or main button
  on the right, descriptions that wrap instead of cutting off, and each
  setting on its own row. Every button is one size, and errors sit on
  the status line rather than floating beside a button.
- **Telegram's status is live.** While the card is open it re-reads the
  connection every few seconds. A poll that drops during a network
  change shows "Reconnecting" in amber and clears on its own once the
  bridge is back, instead of a stale red "unreachable" that stayed until
  the page was reopened. The bridge itself always kept retrying; only
  the card was behind.
