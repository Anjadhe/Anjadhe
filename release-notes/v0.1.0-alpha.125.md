# v0.1.0-alpha.125

Threads become the center of nenva, your registration email carries your
plan, and a way to start over.

## Added

- **Threads are the core.** The nav is Now · Threads · Memory · Settings, in
  the titlebar. Each lasting thing in your life (your auto insurance, a
  child's music classes, a goal) gets one thread that holds its mail, its
  commitments and one conversation; a built-in nenva thread holds general
  chats, routines and private chats. Each thread has an info page with what
  nenva knows about it, and nenva looks at each thing at its own pace,
  raising at most one note in its thread.
- **Your plan follows your email.** Registering again with an address that
  is already registered sends a six-digit code to it; entering the code
  brings back your registration and any plan on it, on a new Mac, after a
  reinstall or after erasing nenva. Settings › Plan shows whose plan it is
  and whether this Mac has confirmed it.
- **Settings › Transfer or reset.** Export and import your memory, and
  **Erase all content and settings**, which moves everything nenva keeps on
  this Mac to the Trash and starts fresh.
- **One commitment to a file and back**, from its page and the Commitments
  list, and Finance's own settings page exports and imports your financial
  records.
- **A gentle first week.** A new install is introduced to what nenva can do
  one thing a day, never on the day you set it up.

## Changed

- **Now shows your day.** Today and Coming up, with nothing to press; every
  card lives in its thread, and anything without a thread of its own is in
  nenva's.
- **Mail makes folders, not tasks.** A folder already holds the thing and
  its next step; a step due today gets its own notice.
- **Cloud AI.** The per-kind cloud privacy switches are gone; Settings ›
  Model is the one choice of where AI runs.
- **Phone pairing is removed from the Mac** while the iPhone app is rebuilt
  as a complete nenva of its own. Data already synced stays on both devices.
- Buying a plan asks you to confirm your email once, so the plan is yours on
  any Mac. The plan code still works, under Settings › Plan › Plan code.

## Fixed

- **Back from a commitment returns where you came from.** A commitment
  opened from a thread's info page went back to its project and then to the
  Commitments list; Back now retraces the way you came.
- A second running copy of nenva no longer freezes or breaks the first.
- Answers on Anthropic models no longer fail with "Invalid signature in
  thinking block" after nenva loads more tools mid-answer.
