# v0.1.0-alpha.126

Threads become home, every thread can show what it tracks, and Finance
becomes something you talk to instead of a set of pages.

## Added

- **What a thread is tracking, drawn.** A chart button in a thread's header
  opens a drawer beside the conversation: a goal's figures over time and
  where it stands, a repeating step's recent days, what is coming up, and
  the amounts on its bills by month. Every number comes from your own
  records. nenva arranges each drawer, and you can rearrange it by saying
  so ("put the days first", "show my bedtime there too").
- **Your money.** A thread listed under Reminders holds your whole money
  picture: net worth and its history, every account, your strategy's check,
  your largest holdings and your spending. Each account, strategy and
  holding has its own thread too, and a thread such as Retirement can hold
  accounts ("my 401k is part of Retirement") and draw them. The money
  coach's notes about your money as a whole now arrive here.
- **Linking and accounts in chat.** "Link my Chase" opens Plaid's own
  sign-in in your browser; when you finish, every account there is added
  and a note in the chat names them. Signing in again, unlinking, starting
  an account you keep by hand, and making a linked account match its
  brokerage are all things you ask for.

## Changed

- **Threads is the home page.** Now is retired: the nav is Threads, Memory
  and Settings, and your day (Today and Coming up) sits at the top of
  nenva's thread, folded until you open it. What nenva is reading right now
  shows on that heading.
- **Finance has no pages.** Its records, bank and brokerage linking, prices
  and your strategy all stay; what the pages showed is in the drawers, and
  what their buttons did is something you ask for. Export and import are
  asked for in chat. Live 15-second quotes went with the pages; prices
  refresh from Yahoo Finance when a drawer showing holdings is open.

## Fixed

- **New news in a thread lights up without a thread switch.** Something that
  comes due on the clock (a "did you do it?" after a repeat, a to-do turning
  overdue) now marks its thread unread within a minute while Threads is open.
- **The Slack morning review works again.** Since the last release nenva
  could not tell which Mac it was on, so a Slack review could not be set up
  ("Could not identify this Mac") and an existing one never ran.
