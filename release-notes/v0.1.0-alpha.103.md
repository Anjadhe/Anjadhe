# v0.1.0-alpha.103

## Changed

- **Portfolio › Strategy opens on an overview.** Each plan is a compact
  card: whether you are on plan, what the plan is for, your mix as one
  bar against its targets, and how many accounts follow it. Click a card
  for the plan's own page, which now reads top to bottom: how it is
  doing and the questions about it, the target mix, the guardrails, then
  the plan's details, accounts, decisions and history.
