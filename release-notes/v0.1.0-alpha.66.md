# v0.1.0-alpha.66

## Added

- **Apple Calendar refreshes on its own.** The mirror of this Mac's
  iCloud and local calendars used to refresh only at launch and on
  "Import now", so an event added in Calendar.app mid-session stayed
  invisible until the next launch. It now refreshes every minute while
  you are looking at the Calendar and every five minutes otherwise, the
  same cadence as Google calendar sync. It reads the Mac's own calendar
  store with no network, and when nothing changed it writes and redraws
  nothing. Reminders and Notes still import at launch and on "Import
  now" only.
- **You can see an update downloading.** While a new build downloads, a
  small strip in the top-right corner under the title bar shows the
  version, a progress bar and how much has arrived. It goes away when
  the download lands and the usual "Update ready" pill takes over. If
  the download fails, the strip says so instead of sitting there.
