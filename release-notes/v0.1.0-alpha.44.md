# v0.1.0-alpha.44

A changelog you can read from the app, and a way to reach the founder.

## Added

- **Changelog in the app.** What's new now has an "Earlier releases" link,
  and About has a Changelog link, that show every release newest first,
  fetched from anjadhe.ai/changelog only when you open it. Offline, the
  shorter list the app carries with it is shown instead.
- **Contact the founder.** A direct email link in About, in Settings ›
  Send Feedback, and in the What's new and Changelog views. Bug reports
  and "I'm lost" messages go straight to the person who wrote the app.

## Changed

- The website's download page can now email you release notes: one email
  per release, no account, unsubscribe deletes your address. The list
  lives on Anjadhe Connect, whose source stays public.
