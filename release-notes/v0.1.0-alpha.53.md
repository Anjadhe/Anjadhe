# v0.1.0-alpha.53

## Added

- **Install and uninstall built-in apps.** The More apps page now carries
  an Uninstall action on packaged apps (Wellness, Pomodoro, Portfolio,
  Bookmarks, News, Reader & Writing Voices, Journal) and a "Not
  installed" section to bring them back. An uninstalled app is truly
  gone — no page, no home card, no search results, and the assistant
  loses its tools too — but its data stays on your Mac and comes back
  intact on install.
- **"Push everything to next week" in one step.** The assistant can now
  shift or collapse many scheduled tasks at once ("move today's tasks to
  tomorrow", "push everything to Monday"), with the exact dates shown
  for approval first. Task lists also show each task's own date.

## Changed

- **Maker is gone.** The experimental artifact builder (off by default
  since early August) has been removed. Building your own apps with a
  coding agent pointed at ~/Anjadhe/apps/ is the build path, and the
  assistant now says so plainly instead of offering builds it can't do.
- **Your model choice is final.** There is no more silent fallback
  between AI engines: a call runs on exactly the brain you picked, and
  if it fails you see that engine's real error instead of your request
  quietly going somewhere else.

## Fixed

- A connection error (ECONNRESET) that could appear on the next message
  right after a streamed local-model reply.
- AI Activity: clicking a row while the page was updating sometimes
  failed to open the detail.
- The Back button now says "Writing Voices" instead of "Library" when
  you came from that app.
