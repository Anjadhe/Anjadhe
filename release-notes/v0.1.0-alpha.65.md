# v0.1.0-alpha.65

## Added

- **Read any article by its link.** Paste a link into the News search
  box and press Enter (or click **Read this link**) and the reader opens
  on it. When the page will not load (a paywall, a bot wall, a consent
  wall), coverage of the same story from other sources is searched and
  summarized instead, and labeled as such. The app now answers
  `anjadhe://read?url=…` links, and a small **Anjadhe Reader** Chrome
  extension (in the source under `extension/chrome`, loaded unpacked for
  now) hands the current tab to the app with one click. It reads nothing
  on the page and sends nothing anywhere else.
- **A business profile for every holding.** Portfolio writes one profile
  per ticker per day: what the company is, what it sells and to whom,
  financial soundness, whether it looks expensive or cheap, risks, and a
  bottom line, for funds in a fund shape. Every claim comes from a fact
  sheet of Yahoo Finance figures plus cached headlines; the model may cite
  nothing else. Tell the assistant when it has something wrong and the
  correction is saved as a note on that ticker, and today's profile is
  rewritten around it.
- **A daily market-and-portfolio brief** at the top of All Accounts: the
  market (indexes, yields, VIX, commodities), your book since a week, a
  month and the start of the year, largest positions, movers, how you
  stand against your plan, and dated headlines. It describes and never
  advises. **Rewrite now** refreshes it on demand, and the assistant reads
  the same brief when you ask about your day.
- **Reach your Mac from anywhere.** With Tailscale on the Mac, the new
  **Reach from anywhere** switch in Settings › Paired Devices publishes
  this Mac's own relay on its Tailscale name, so your phone reaches this
  Mac directly from any network with nothing installed on the phone.
  Anjadhe Connect's relay stays the fallback.

## Changed

- **Forwarded notifications carry a summary, not a bare time.** Reminders
  sent to Telegram or iMessage now include the date, description, linked
  project, source email and tags; routine notices name the routine and
  the outcome; email insight notices add the folder, due date and amount.
  The header names only the kind ("Reminder", "Task"), without an
  "Anjadhe ·" prefix, since the bot and the handle already say who is
  sending.

## Fixed

- **Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders never asked for permission.** On
  macOS 14 and later the app requests full access, and the packaged app
  was missing the usage description that request requires, so macOS
  refused silently: no consent dialog, and no Anjadhe row under Privacy &
  Security. Turning on Apple Calendar now shows the dialog.
