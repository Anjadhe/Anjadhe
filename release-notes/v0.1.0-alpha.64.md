# v0.1.0-alpha.64

## Changed

- **News never leaves your Mac's address when you use Anjadhe Connect.**
  Yesterday's fix for blank Portfolio news made the app fetch headlines
  directly from Google, from this Mac, whenever Anjadhe Connect could
  not. That sent your topics, and in Portfolio your holdings, from your
  own address, which is exactly what choosing Connect avoids. That
  fallback is gone. When Connect cannot fetch, News and Portfolio say so
  plainly, keep the last headlines they did fetch, and fetch nothing
  from here instead; the assistant is told the same when you ask about
  a stock. If you use your own Tavily or Brave key, headlines still come
  straight from your Mac, as before. Connect itself now has a second
  news source so the outage that started this is absorbed on the server.
