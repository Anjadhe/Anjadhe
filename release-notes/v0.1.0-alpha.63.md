# v0.1.0-alpha.63

## Fixed

- **News on holdings went blank.** Portfolio's ticker pages and account
  pages stopped showing headlines, and the News app could fall silent,
  when Anjadhe Connect's news feed could not reach its source. Connect
  answered per topic with an empty list, and the app took that empty
  answer as the truth. Now any topic Connect could not fetch is fetched
  from this Mac directly, Portfolio keeps the headlines it already has
  rather than replacing them with nothing, and an empty result is
  re-checked after five minutes instead of thirty.
