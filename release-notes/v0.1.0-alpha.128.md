# v0.1.0-alpha.128

Commitments export and import as a whole, mail lands in the right thread
more often, and Reminders works both ways.

## Added

- **Export and import all your commitments.** The foot of the Commitments
  list has **Export all commitments…** (every commitment, open and finished,
  in one file) and **Import commitments…**, which reads a whole export or a
  single commitment, with one confirm and Undo. The assistant can do both.

## Changed

- **A to-do can go back to Reminders.** Ask the assistant to move a to-do
  back to Reminders and it stays there; Undo puts it back where it was.
- **Links inside a thread open the page.** In Reminders every to-do is
  captioned, and the caption opens the to-do's own page. A commitment link
  inside its own thread opens its page instead of reopening the thread.
- **Memory is shorter.** The Your money and email and texts rows are gone:
  both are threads on Threads already.

## Fixed

- **Mail is placed by who sent it.** When deciding which thread a message
  belongs to, nenva now sees who each thread's mail comes from, and asks
  again before joining a message to a thread whose senders do not match. A
  cloud console notice no longer lands in an unrelated account's thread.
- **Reminders no longer disappears.** A thread named Reminders could be made
  beside the built-in one and take over its to-dos.
- **Placing kept running.** After a pass with nothing to place, new things
  waited until the next launch to be placed in a thread.
