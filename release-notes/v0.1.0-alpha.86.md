# v0.1.0-alpha.86

## Changed

- **Anjadhe is now nenva.** Same app, same company (Anjadhe LLC), new name,
  written in lowercase. The menu bar, the Dock, the window, Settings, Help,
  notifications and the assistant all say nenva now, and the hosted services
  are nenva Cloud and nenva Connect. Nothing about how the app works has
  changed: your data, your folders, your license, your connected accounts,
  your paired phone and sync between your Macs all carry on as they were.
- **A new icon.** A white lowercase n with a leaf growing from it, on the
  same dark tile.
- **The website moved to nenva.co.** Old anjadhe.ai links forward there.
  The support and founder email addresses have not changed.
- **Earlier releases open on GitHub.** The changelog page on the website is
  gone; What's new and the update notice link to the full list of releases
  instead.

## Fixed

- **Help said AI runs on your Mac by default.** A new install starts on
  nenva Cloud; the help now says so, and says where to switch.

## Good to know

- **The app in Applications keeps its old name until you rename it.** An
  update replaces the app in place, so on this Mac it is still called
  Anjadhe in Applications and in Finder, while everything inside it says
  nenva. Rename it if you like; nothing depends on the name. A fresh
  download is called nenva.
- **Your files are still in the Anjadhe folder** in your home directory
  (Notes, Journal, Documents, your apps). It was not moved, so anything that
  points at it, like an Obsidian vault, keeps working.
