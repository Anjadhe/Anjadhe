# v0.1.0-alpha.55

## Added

- **Your voice takes center stage in Reader.** The Writing Voices home
  now IS your own voice's page: enable it, pick what it may learn from —
  your Notes, Journal, sent emails, or documents you add — and Study
  builds a style guide with real exemplars of your writing. Sent mail is
  studied only where you wrote it (quotes, forwards and signatures are
  stripped), and AI-written feed posts are excluded from "My notes".
  Other voices live behind one quiet door below.
- **From email, in the Tasks nav.** A new Other filters section slices
  the list to every task the email pipeline created; an email-born
  task's detail also names its source message (from, subject, the
  insight summary) so "what is this about?" is answerable in place.
- **Emails about attending something become tasks.** A validated dated
  attend-event (a webinar invite, a school event) now files as an
  appointment and lands on your schedule — attending is the action.
- **Wellness activities carry distance**, and talking about a run or a
  ride in chat brings the Wellness tools along.

## Changed

- **Goals are now called Projects** — everywhere in the app, the
  assistant and help. Only the name changed: your records, links and
  saved reviews carry over, and typing "goal" to the assistant (or
  `@goal`) still works.
- **Setup is three steps.** The wizard's claims now read as scannable
  fact rows; the sync button moved to Settings › Storage & Backup (the
  one place that consent lives), and the web-access step is gone —
  instead, the assistant's very first web search asks you in the moment,
  with the terms stated in the approval dialog.
- The Email AI overview uses the full window width, and a task opened
  from an Email AI list gets a back chip naming where you came from.

## Fixed

- The AI brain you picked is never silently replaced by the engine
  check; a failing model now shows that engine's real error.
