# v0.1.0-alpha.54

A quick fix release on the heels of alpha.53.

## Fixed

- **Assistant page styling.** alpha.53 shipped with a broken stylesheet
  that left parts of the AI Assistant page unstyled — routine-run chips,
  task cards and composer states rendered as bare text. All restored.
- The assistant's welcome message no longer offers to build documents
  and small apps (that was Maker, removed in alpha.53); it names
  voice-drafted writing instead.
