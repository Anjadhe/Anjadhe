# v0.1.0-alpha.32

Every app is now one click away, all the time: a sidebar down the left of
the window.

## Added

- **A sidebar listing every app.** It runs down the left of the window,
  grouped by what each app is for, and marks the one you are in. Home is
  its first row. It shows exactly what the titlebar's app grid shows —
  hide an app in **Customize** and it leaves both, while staying
  findable by name in Cmd+K.
- **Collapse it when you want the room.** The **Collapse** control at the
  foot of the sidebar narrows it to icons and keeps it that way until
  you expand it again. The choice is per-Mac, like the theme. On a
  narrow window it collapses on its own, and on a very narrow one it
  steps out of the way entirely — Cmd+K and the titlebar grid still
  reach everything.

## Changed

- **The help the assistant answers from covers getting around.** Asking
  it how to move between apps now describes the sidebar, the wordmark as
  the way home, and the back button, instead of only Cmd+K.
