# v0.1.0-alpha.123

Now tells you what is really on your day, and the About page makes way
for the website.

## Changed

- **The line at the top of Now says what is true.** It no longer says
  "Nothing needs you right now" when you have meetings today. It names a
  meeting you are in and when it ends, what is next and how many more
  follow ("Next up: Design review at 3:00 PM, then 2 more."), what is still
  on today without a time, and the cards waiting below ("Below: a heads-up
  and 2 check-ins."). It says nothing needs you only when that is so. The
  phone shows the same line.
- **The About page is gone.** It had fallen out of date. Settings › About
  keeps your version and updates, Send feedback and Registration, and its
  last row opens nenva.co, which says what nenva is and links the
  changelog, terms and privacy policy.
