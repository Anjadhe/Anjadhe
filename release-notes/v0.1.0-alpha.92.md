# v0.1.0-alpha.92

## Added

- **The iPhone app works when your Mac is away** (iPhone preview, paired
  under Settings, Paired Devices). Tasks, News and Portfolio are built on
  the phone itself when it cannot reach your Mac, and each page says so.
  Your Mac still answers whenever it can.
- **A second model for your phone.** Under Settings, AI Assistant, Models
  (or in the phone's own Settings), choose nenva Cloud for when your Mac is
  away. It is off until you choose it. The phone then answers by itself,
  reading the web and your synced tasks, calendar, projects and notes,
  changing nothing, and it names the model under every answer. It uses your
  Mac's nenva Cloud allowance.
- **Approvals on your phone.** When a chat from the phone needs your OK for
  a step, the question now comes to the phone, with the same choices as on
  the Mac, instead of being declined.

## Changed

- **One conversation list across your Macs and your phone.** Conversations
  now sync one by one: a chat continued on two devices keeps both sides'
  messages, and a chat on one Mac no longer replaces the other Mac's.
  Which chat is open is remembered per device.
