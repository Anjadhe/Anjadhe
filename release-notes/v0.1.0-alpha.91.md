# v0.1.0-alpha.91

## Added

- **Calendar events in search.** Cmd+K finds your calendar events, and so
  does the assistant when it searches across your records. A repeating event
  shows up once, as its next occurrence, and a result opens the event.

## Fixed

- **Journal entries were hard to find in search.** Cmd+K and the assistant's
  search matched journal entries only by their title or date, not what you
  wrote, and a result could not open the right entry. Both work again.
