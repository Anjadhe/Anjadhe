# v0.1.0-alpha.76

## Changed

- **The left sidebar sits on the page, not on a white column.** Plain
  stroked icons, the app you are in marked by a bar at the rail's edge
  and its name in ink, everything else quiet. The rounded icon tiles from
  the release before last are gone: this theme keeps white for the things
  you read, and a column of boxed icons beside them read as furniture at
  the same level as your content.
- **Projects uses the width it has.** The table starts at the left edge of
  the page heading instead of sitting inset from it, and a project's group
  moved out from under its title into its own quiet column, a dot and the
  name. Clicking it still filters to that group. The column only appears
  when the table spans more than one group.
- **The news reader fills its column** rather than sitting as a narrow
  island inside a wide page.
- **What the assistant tells you about the app is up to date.** Its help
  answers now name Cloud Privacy, Contacts and the "More apps" row by the
  labels they actually carry, describe project updates rather than goal
  updates, say News starts enabled, and speak to you in the second person
  throughout.
- **Local AI is described honestly by memory size.** Any Mac with 8 GB or
  more runs the model on the machine: Anjadhe's own small Qwen model on
  8 to 16 GB, larger open-weight models from 32 GB up. The help and the
  README no longer imply 32 GB is the entry point.

## Added

- **The iPhone app wears the Mac's theme**, token for token: the same
  ground and white sheets, ink and greys, one blue for what is live, pill
  buttons, and headings in a serif at regular weight. Its bundled display
  font is gone, since the theme uses the system faces.
