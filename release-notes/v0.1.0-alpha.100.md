# v0.1.0-alpha.100

## Changed

- **Updates download quietly.** The download bar that showed over every
  page while a new version downloaded is gone. **Settings › App version**
  (under Support) now shows the version you have and what the update is
  doing: downloading, with its progress, ready to install with
  **Restart & update**, or up to date. It also has **Check for updates**
  and links to the release notes. The titlebar "Update ready" pill and
  the Home card still appear once a new version is ready.
