# v0.1.0-alpha.85

## Changed

- **Portfolio and News on your phone, in full.** The paired iPhone app now
  shows the real apps rather than a summary of them: accounts and scopes,
  every ticker with its indicators, a ticker's own page with its business
  profile, your strategy and the daily brief; and in News, the rail, the
  lead card, Catch me up, topics, and the reader with its summary, related
  coverage and source. Every number and every headline is still worked out
  on your Mac and sent to the phone to display — the phone computes nothing
  of its own and holds nothing you have not already synced.
- **Replies arrive as they are written.** Asking your Mac something from the
  phone used to sit silent until the whole answer was ready; it now streams
  in as it comes.
- **Apps is a place on the phone**, the fourth tab, instead of a row of pills
  on the home screen, and an answer reads as full-width prose rather than a
  chat bubble.

## Fixed

- **"Contact the founder" in What's new did nothing.** It handed a `mailto:`
  to macOS, which opens nothing at all if you have no mail client set up. It
  now opens anjadhe.ai/founder — the same address, plus who he is and the
  other ways to reach him — which is what Settings › Send Feedback has done
  since alpha.82.
