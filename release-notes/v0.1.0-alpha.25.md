# v0.1.0-alpha.25

This release is about the planning side of Anjadhe getting simpler and more
honest. Focus areas are gone, replaced by a plain group label on each goal.
Your portfolio plan became a page you can actually read, with the numbers
computed rather than narrated. Scheduled prompts are now called routines and
have a proper table. And the assistant got substantially better at finishing
multi-step work instead of claiming it did.

## Added

- **A Plan page for your portfolio.** The strategy used to live only in
  conversation. Now **Plan** in the accounts nav shows it: the plan in your
  own words, a target mix table with each sleeve's target against actual, a
  bar marking the tolerance band, and what to trim or add in dollars to get
  back on target. Under it, your guardrails with a pass or fail beside each,
  and a one-line verdict: On plan, Drifting, or Off plan. Every number is
  computed by the app with the same arithmetic the assistant uses, so the
  page and the chat cannot disagree. Nothing on the page is a form: anything
  that changes the plan is a button that hands the question to the
  assistant, and **Ask about this strategy** opens a chat with that plan
  already in context.
- **Weekday reviews of a plan, beside the plan.** "Review it every weekday
  morning" schedules a routine, and its latest read is annotated in the
  margin next to the strategy it is about. Goals work the same way with a
  weekly review.
- **Goals can be deleted, and so can strategies.** A plan you no longer
  follow can be removed from its card, and the assistant can do it too.
- **Quick-add understands you.** Type a task in plain language in Actions
  and the assistant can turn it into a proper task with a date, a repeat,
  and a goal.
- **An engine update when there is one.** When a newer llama.cpp build is
  available, Settings, AI Assistant, Your Models shows an Update engine row.
  It installs the same way the first one did.
- **Search finds your portfolio accounts**, both in Cmd+K and for the
  assistant.

## Changed

- **Focus areas are gone; goals carry a group label.** There is no longer a
  set of focus areas to create, colour and maintain. Each goal has a plain
  Group field ("Health", "Work") and the Goals page groups by it. A group
  exists because a goal carries its name. Your existing focus areas were
  converted to group labels automatically, on every Mac.
- **The Plan tab is now the Goals tab**, and it shows one group at a time
  rather than every goal at once.
- **A goal is completed or it is not.** The four working statuses collapsed
  to one honest pair. Progress lives in the goal's tasks, and the weekly AI
  review is where you get a read on whether it is actually moving. Feeling
  stuck opens a conversation instead of setting a label.
- **Scheduled prompts are called routines.** Same feature, a word people
  actually use. The Routines page is routines only and is now a sortable,
  filterable, searchable table. Prompts you run by hand rather than on a
  schedule went back to Notes as a saved prompt template.
- **Goal, task and routine details are quiet property lists.** Labels in a
  gutter, hairline rows, fields that reveal themselves as inputs when you
  hover. Detail pages read as one column now, with the heading at the top of
  it.
- **The assistant finishes what it starts.** Multi-step tasks now plan in a
  checked shape, replan around a step that failed rather than stopping,
  verify against the goal you actually asked for rather than against the
  steps it invented, and always deliver something at the end. Tasks can read
  attached images and files, keep their card when you navigate away, and
  carry the conversation they came from.
- **One search in the app.** Notes lost its own search box; Cmd+K is the
  search.
- **Task lists fold completed rows** behind a collapsed toggle, and tasks
  with no time say "anytime" instead of showing a made-up hour.
- **A task's goal is a link.** The goal name on a task opens it, and the ×
  beside it is how you change it.
- **Settings permission rows are one line each**, and the Automations page
  rows are compact with their actions on hover.
- **The composer mode chip is gone.** There is one assistant, and it decides
  how to handle what you ask.
- Portfolio remembers how you sorted a table, and holdings show day-change
  percent.
- The home feed lost its preferences button; Unread and All is the whole
  control.

## Fixed

- The assistant no longer says a task is running when it is not.
- Email bundles no longer count rolled-up messages as unread, so the Email
  card's count matches what you see when you open it.
- A goal renamed anywhere carries its review routine along, instead of
  leaving an orphaned routine pointing at the old name.
- Long search queries from a task step are rejected rather than failing
  silently, and a step that only narrates what it would do no longer counts
  as done.
