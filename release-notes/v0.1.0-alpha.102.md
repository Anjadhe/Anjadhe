# v0.1.0-alpha.102

## Changed

- **nenva is free, for good.** Everything in the app today stays free,
  with no account. The one-time license that was planned for after the
  alpha is gone, and so is its trial: every install gets every update.
  Some apps added later may be paid, such as ones that connect to outside
  services, and nenva cloud AI may become a separate subscription. Today
  nenva cloud comes with a free monthly allowance, as does web search.
- **A free license for everyone.** **Settings › License** (also on the
  Home checklist) gives anyone a free license, not only alpha users. The
  email you enter is how nenva reaches you: release news and important
  notices, with an unsubscribe link in every email. It also gets you the
  same license back on another Mac. If you claimed an alpha license
  before, it still works and nothing changes for you.
- **nenva is one Mac and your iPhone.** Syncing between Macs is retired:
  each Mac that runs nenva now keeps its own data, and the iPhone app
  keeps working through your Mac as before. Nothing is deleted; the sync
  files in your iCloud Drive stay where they are. To move to a new Mac,
  restore a backup there.
- **Backup Passphrase.** What was Sync Encryption in **Settings › Storage
  & Backup** now protects your backups: with your passphrase a backup
  opens on any Mac. If a Mac ends up on a different key from the one your
  passphrase protects, nenva now notices and says so, and **Check
  passphrase** puts it right without losing the backups it already made.
- **Check for Updates opens Settings › App version.** The menu item now
  takes you to the page that shows the check, the download and
  **Restart & update**, instead of a passing notice.
