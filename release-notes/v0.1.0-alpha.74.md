# v0.1.0-alpha.74

## Added

- **Choose where your news comes from.** The News app's Topics page has
  a new **Sources** card: Google News (on by default), Bing News and
  Hacker News. Every topic you follow is fetched from each source you
  turn on, and the stories merge into the same timeline. A story from a
  source other than Google News says so in its byline, and a Hacker News
  story shows its points and, in the reader, a **Discussion** link to
  the thread. Nothing changes until you turn a second source on.
- **The assistant sees the source too.** `get_news` reports which source
  a headline came from and its Hacker News points, so a morning digest
  can say so.

## Changed

- **Anjadhe Connect users' picks travel through Connect** like their
  topics always have. Their sources are fetched from Anjadhe's server,
  never from the Mac, and a source that is down leaves the others'
  stories standing. Until Connect ships the matching update it serves
  Google News only; the app handles that quietly.
