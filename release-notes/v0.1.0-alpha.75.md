# v0.1.0-alpha.75

## Added

- **Projects open on one table.** Every active project sits in a single
  table with its progress, next open step, last update and target date
  (red once passed, amber within a week). A **Needs attention** list above
  it names what wants you: a target passed or due this week, a project with
  no open next step, one that has gone quiet for two weeks. Completed
  projects fold beneath. The left rail is gone; the pills under the title
  filter by group, and each group keeps its own colour on chips and
  progress bars. Pick a group and click its title to rename it, or Remove
  group to dissolve it (its projects stay).
- **Updates, a log on every project and task.** Post what happened ("sent
  the deck, waiting on Dana") and the assistant posts there too: its read
  when you ask how a project is going or press **Ask Anjadhe to check
  in**, a risk it noticed, and every weekly review. Each entry says who
  posted it and when, and the page shows how long since the last one.
  Updates are history, not instructions; a standing instruction is still a
  Decision. **Mark complete** on a project now asks what to do with its
  open tasks.
- **Portfolio: a Tickers page.** A new **Tickers** section in the left nav
  puts every symbol you hold or follow on one sortable, filterable table.
  A ticker held in three accounts is one row; a ticker you hold and watch
  is one row with a Watching chip. Position columns read "—" on a
  watch-only row.
- **Portfolio: verdict tiles on a ticker profile.** The profile now ends in
  a Verdicts section (valuation, soundness, AI role, near- and long-term
  risk and value, news tone), and the page shows them as tiles above the
  prose. They are the model's own summary of what the prose says, never
  arithmetic, and never shown without the text that justifies them. Older
  profiles are rewritten once to the new shape.
- **Portfolio sharing, phase one, behind a flag.** Share one Portfolio
  account read-only with another Anjadhe user. Pair by invite code under
  **Settings › Accounts › Contacts**, then use the Share door on an
  account; the copy arrives under a "Shared with you" section on their
  Mac, excluded from their totals, and revoking removes it. Everything
  travels end to end encrypted through Anjadhe's relay, which sees only
  ciphertext. Both Macs have to be online for now. Off by default: turn on
  **Sharing** under Settings › Developer › Experimental features.

## Changed

- **A new look, matching the website.** The app now wears the same theme
  as anjadhe.ai: no web font (system sans for text, the OS serif for
  headings at regular weight), a compact type scale, an off-white ground
  with white panels, pill buttons and inputs, one blue reserved for what
  is live (focus, attention counts, the AI tag). The things you read (a
  routine result, a chat, a note, a journal entry, a document, a news
  story, an email, an insight) sit on a white sheet at one prose size.
- **A new app icon.** A white serif A, in the same face as the app's
  headings, on a near-black tile. No dot, no colour.
- **News is enabled by default again** for new installs. If you turned it
  off, it stays off.

## Fixed

- **A document's tags could come back after you removed the document.**
  Deleting a document moments after tagging or renaming its tags left the
  removal stamped a hair behind the last edit, so the next sync could
  restore the tags. The removal now always outranks the edit. (The
  eval journey that caught it was blamed on leftovers the day before; the
  Wellness package journey was also updated because News is enabled by
  default again.)
