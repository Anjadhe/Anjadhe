# v0.1.0-alpha.17

## Added

- **How Anjadhe works, in the app**: the About view now tells the full story
  from the website (a canvas of apps, your data on your own SSD, the privacy
  promise, and only then the AI), so you can read it without leaving the app.

## Changed

- **Email insight cards read like inbox rows**: the sender leads the card,
  the subject is a real title instead of an uppercase chip, the AI summary
  sits quietly underneath, and each action item is its own row with a
  due-date chip that turns red once the date has passed.
- **Feed cards show their source at a glance**: a subtle accent stripe on the
  left edge marks email insights (teal) and prompt results (purple); posts
  from Anjadhe stay neutral.
- **Dates read like a person wrote them**: insight titles, summaries, and
  action items show "Jul 31" instead of "2026-07-31", everywhere insights
  appear.
- **The setup checklist got out of the way**: the "Set up more of Anjadhe"
  banner above the chat box is now a quiet pill in the home page's bottom
  corner. Click it to continue, or dismiss it with the ×.
- **"Push to today" moved next to the Overdue heading** on the Actions and
  Tasks pages, instead of sitting in the far corner.
- **Slimmer home feed header**: prompt creation lives on the Prompts page
  (its + New Prompt button); the feed header keeps preferences, Clear all,
  and Manage prompts.
- **Notes: + New Note is a plain button again**: every new note starts blank,
  and the editor's Template control switches it in place. The dropdown caret
  is gone.
- **Notes: Show-on-Home removed**: the home page no longer renders pinned
  notes, so the house-icon toggle and the On Home filter did nothing and are
  gone.

## Fixed

- **Email insights appear on home from first launch**: previously the feed
  stayed empty until you visited the Email app once. Mail sync and analysis
  now start at launch, and newly analyzed insights appear on the home feed
  live, without navigating or refreshing.
- **Background AI waits for a configured model**: email analysis and bundle
  classification used to fire doomed calls when no model was set, littering
  AI Activity with errors. They now wait quietly and catch up the moment you
  pick a model.
