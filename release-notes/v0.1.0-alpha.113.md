# v0.1.0-alpha.113

Attach what a commitment is about, suggestions instead of surprise work
sessions, and a little colour on Now.

## Added

- **Attach to a commitment.** On a commitment's page, Attach finds a file
  or text document by name, takes a pasted link (a Notion page, a form),
  uploads a new file into Documents, or takes files dropped on the page.
  Each opens from the commitment, and every attach has Undo. The assistant
  can attach these too, and reads them when you ask about the commitment.
- **Save a chat attachment to Documents.** Ask the assistant to keep a file
  you attached in a chat; it is copied into Documents and tagged.
- **Add files on the Documents page**, or drop them on it; while a tag is
  chosen, what lands wears it.
- **Insights search reads more**: folder titles, status, next steps, facts,
  message summaries and trip briefs.
- **Your phone matches the Mac**: Now, Chats, Memory and Settings, with
  Coming up on Now and a card's box opening that thing's own chat.

## Changed

- **Work time is suggested, never scheduled for you.** For something due
  soon, nenva suggests sessions on the commitment and on Now with one
  button, Schedule, and Not needed; other times are agreed in its chat.
- **Coming up** is folded to its heading until you open it, then shows the
  next thing and the week as a strip of days.
- **Now and the left nav have a soft blue** and tinted cards, flat in light
  and dark.

## Fixed

- A commitment's note from nenva no longer takes a "tomorrow" you said
  yesterday as tomorrow still; it knows the day you said it.
- A phone change could stay waiting to sync forever after a relaunch.
