# v0.1.0-alpha.105

## Added

- **The assistant prepares things for you.** It now does a little work
  ahead of you, and you only look or approve:
  - **Meeting prep.** About 30 minutes before a meeting with other people,
    a notification says who it is with, the last email you exchanged with
    them, and what to have ready. Home's "On your radar" shows the same.
  - **A morning notice.** Once each morning: today's meetings, tasks due
    and overdue, and follow-ups waiting. A quiet morning sends nothing.
  - **Follow-up drafts.** When an email you sent a few days ago asked
    something and nobody replied, a short nudge is drafted for you on Home
    under **Needs you**. **Review draft** opens the reply ready to edit;
    nothing is ever sent for you. Say **Not now** to the ones you don't
    want, and it learns.
- **Experimental: Open threads.** When the assistant could not finish
  something in a chat, it can note what was in the way, so it can come
  back when that changes. Off by default, and nothing is shown yet; turn
  it on in **Settings › Developer › Experimental features**.

## Changed

- **Clicking your plan in Portfolio opens it.** The strategy in the top
  card on All Accounts and on each account now opens that plan's own page.
