# v0.1.0-alpha.61

## Changed

- **A new app icon.** A simple white "A" on a dark grey tile replaces the
  old drawing, in the Dock, on the iPhone app and on anjadhe.ai. Inside
  the app the logo now appears only on the loading screen; the first-run
  welcome, About and the assistant's first-visit card no longer show it.
- **No price quoted.** About, What's new, the help article and Settings ›
  License no longer name a future license price. The plan is unchanged:
  free during the alpha, a one-time license with a year of updates and no
  account planned later, and everyone who installs during the alpha keeps
  the app free for good.
