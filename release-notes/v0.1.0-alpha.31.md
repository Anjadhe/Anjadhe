# v0.1.0-alpha.31

New email reaches Anjadhe within a minute, and chats pinned to a task or
goal stop losing track of their subject.

## Changed

- **New mail shows up within a minute.** Anjadhe used to slow its Gmail
  checks to every 3–10 minutes while you were away from the Mac — which
  is exactly when a confirmation email lands in Gmail and sits invisible
  here. It now checks every minute for as long as the app is running
  (each check is one tiny "anything new?" call), and checks immediately
  when the Mac wakes from sleep.
- **The About view carries the sharpened pitch.** The privacy promise
  section gains the breach/subpoena line and the unplug-the-network
  test, and the footer tagline becomes the ownership line. The footer
  also links the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy — the app never
  linked its own terms anywhere before.
- **The Strategy page says what it is not.** A quiet line at the foot of
  the strategy list and detail states Anjadhe is not an investment
  adviser, matching the new investment-tools clause in the terms.

## Fixed

- **Chats attached to a record keep their subject.** A conversation
  pinned to a task, goal, note, routine, strategy or account answered
  "which task are you referring to?" when continued from the Assistant
  view. It now rebuilds the record's live context wherever the chat is
  continued. Chats over untrusted content (incoming mail, web pages)
  deliberately do not.
- **A failed mail check can no longer silently stop mail checking** for
  the rest of the session — the next check is always scheduled, even
  when one fails.
- **Release pipeline:** the asset-verify pass now runs and repairs a
  partial publish regardless of the build tool's exit code (alpha.30's
  own publish needed a manual rerun for exactly this).
