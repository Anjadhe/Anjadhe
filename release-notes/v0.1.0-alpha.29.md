# v0.1.0-alpha.29

A small release that opens the strategy record up and makes the app a
better citizen of Anjadhe Cloud's rate limits — plus a privacy pass over
the app's own console logs.

## Added

- **Every strategy has its own page.** Clicking a plan's name on the
  Strategy page opens the whole record: the computed verdict, every saved
  property, the target mix and guardrails, the accounts actually following
  the plan (each one a link to that account), and the plan's change
  history — recorded since the first save, shown for the first time. The
  question pills underneath fit the plan's state: a draft offers to be
  finished, a drifting plan offers the way back, a plan nobody follows
  offers to be assigned.

## Changed

- **Anjadhe Cloud rate limits are paced, not fought.** When the cloud
  says "slow down", background email analysis now pauses exactly as long
  as asked and resumes where it left off, instead of burning its retries
  into a closed window. Scheduled routines treat a rate limit as "try
  again shortly" rather than posting an error card to your feed.
- **The app's own logs no longer quote your content.** Console output
  (visible when Anjadhe is launched from a terminal, and capturable by
  anything that redirects it) used to include snippets of prompts, model
  replies, email subjects, and resolved article URLs. Those lines now log
  lengths and shapes only; the full traces still exist for debugging but
  are off unless explicitly enabled.
- **A brand-new install's first briefing welcomes you** instead of
  reporting that nothing is due, nothing is overdue, and there are no
  goals — it says what will land there each morning and suggests a first
  step.

## Fixed

- Linking to a note whose title was empty (possible for notes created on
  the phone) no longer breaks the link picker's search.
