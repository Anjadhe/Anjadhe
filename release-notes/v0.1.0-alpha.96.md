# v0.1.0-alpha.96

## Added

- **Live prices while the US market is open.** From 9:30 AM to 4 PM New
  York time on weekdays, wherever you are, US stocks and ETFs on the
  Portfolio page update every 15 seconds, and a row tints green or red for
  a moment when its price moves. The caption at the top reads **Live** with
  the time of the last update. The quotes come from Robinhood's public
  quote service in one request for all your symbols, with no account or
  sign-in. Mutual funds, non-US listings and options are not on that
  service and keep updating from Yahoo Finance every 5 minutes. Nothing is
  fetched while you are on another page, the window is hidden, or the
  market is closed.
