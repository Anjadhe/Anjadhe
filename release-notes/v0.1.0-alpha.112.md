# v0.1.0-alpha.112

Plans arrive in Settings, routines live in their own chats, and the
assistant is the one place to ask how nenva works.

## Added

- **Settings › Plan.** This month's nenva cloud use as a percentage, with
  the share that went to background work (reading your email, routines),
  and web searches used out of the month's allowance, each with when it
  resets. When paid plans open on our side, this is where you choose one
  (Plus or Pro, monthly or yearly; you pay on Stripe's page in your
  browser, which shows the price first), enter a plan code from another
  Mac or your receipt, manage or cancel a subscription, and add a one-time
  top-up. Your own search key is never counted, and nenva local has no
  monthly limit.
- **When an allowance runs out, nenva says so.** A notice at 80% and once
  when it is used, and a plain line in the chat with when it resets and
  what you can do now. nenva never switches you to another model by itself.
- **Notion, Linear and Slack connectors**, read when you ask (Settings ›
  Connectors). Nothing is imported in the background.
- **Charts and tables in answers.** The assistant can show a table or a
  chart drawn from what a tool returned (Finance first); it never supplies
  the numbers itself.
- **A page for every folder.** A bill, appointment, order or reservation
  nenva keeps from your mail and texts has its own page with every message
  in it and its own chat. Insights is the list of those folders.
- **One Documents page** for everything nenva keeps that you can read: what
  the assistant wrote, what you wrote, and your files.
- **Memory is in the left nav**: commitments, folders from mail, Finance,
  Documents and what nenva remembers about you.
- **The Mac stays awake for scheduled work** while routines are armed, on
  battery too; the display still sleeps, and Pause scheduled routines lets
  the Mac sleep.

## Changed

- **Routines live in their own chats.** A routine is a chat under Standing
  on the Chats page; each result it writes is a message there (the last
  ten are kept) and you can reply to any of them. Opening a result opens
  its chat at that message. Your routines and their past results move
  over on first launch, with a backup; they no longer appear as notes or
  as files in ~/nenva/Documents.
- **The assistant is the help.** The Help page and the "?" buttons are
  gone; Help in the menu bar opens a new chat, and the assistant answers
  from nenva's own guide.
- **One lock.** Per-app locks are retired; Lock nenva (Touch ID) in
  Settings › Privacy covers the app. A Mac that had apps locked turns it on.
- **One ledger.** The AI Activity page is gone; What left this Mac also
  lists work kept on this Mac. The logs moved to Settings › Advanced ›
  Developer.
- **Mail opens in Gmail.** The in-app inbox is gone; reading and analysis
  stay.
- **Calmer Chats, routine pages and Insights.** Chats has Conversations and
  Standing tabs; a routine's page is a masthead over plain rows.
- The nenva browser extension reloads itself when the app is newer.
- Retired: the More apps page, building your own apps, the insight report,
  Reset insights, model-written suggested prompts, and forwarding
  notifications by iMessage.
