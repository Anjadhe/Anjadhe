# v0.1.0-alpha.98

## Changed

- **This app is nenva again.** The name and the sprout n icon are back
  everywhere you read them: the Dock, the menu bar, Settings, Help,
  notifications, nenva Cloud and nenva Connect, the Chrome extensions and
  the iPhone app. Your data, accounts and settings carry over.
- **Your folders say nenva too.** On first launch, nenva renames its folders
  in place. Nothing is copied or deleted:
  - `~/Anjadhe` (your notes, journal, documents and apps) becomes `~/nenva`
  - the app's internal data becomes `~/Library/Application Support/nenva`
  - the local AI engine and models become `~/.nenva_llamacpp`
  - the iCloud Drive sync and backup folders become `.nenva_sync` and
    `.nenva_backup`

  Links are left at the old names on your Mac, so anything that remembered
  an old path keeps working. iCloud Drive does not sync links, so a Mac
  still on an older version keeps syncing to the old iCloud folder until it
  updates too. Update every Mac you sync.
- **Saved keys carry over on their own.** nenva now keeps its secrets
  under a "nenva Safe Storage" Keychain item. Your API keys, Google and
  Telegram sign-ins and the sync key are moved over from the old
  "Anjadhe Safe Storage" item automatically on first launch.
