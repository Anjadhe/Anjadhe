# v0.1.0-alpha.79

## Added

- **Private chats.** Click **Private** in the AI Assistant header (or
  **Start a private chat** in the docked panel's ⋯ menu) for a chat the
  assistant won't remember. It isn't saved to your chat history or synced
  to your other Macs, nothing from it goes into memory, and LLM Logs and AI
  Activity record only that a request happened, not what it said. It ends
  when you leave it — End, a new conversation, another chat, or quitting
  the app (which asks first). A **Use my personal info** checkbox on the
  chat's banner starts off; tick it to let the assistant read your tasks,
  notes, calendar and memory for that chat, still without remembering
  anything. Things you ask it to do in a private chat, like creating a task,
  still happen. Your words still go to the model you picked.

## Fixed

- **Browser screenshots now reach the model.** When the assistant took a
  screenshot in the Browser (Playwright) tool, the image was saved to a file
  and never shown to the model, so it fell back to reading the page's code.
  A model that can read images now sees the screenshot it asked for.
- **Tasks on your accounts use your own browser.** With **Use other apps**
  turned on, a request that involves your accounts ("add it to my cart",
  "check out", "sign in", "open the browser") now runs in your own
  signed-in browser instead of the separate Playwright browser, which is
  signed in to nothing and used to report your cart as empty. Reading public
  pages can still use either.
