# v0.1.0-alpha.72

## Added

- **Insights from your texts.** Turn on **Settings › Accounts › iMessage ›
  Insights from your texts** and the texts that arrive in Messages on this
  Mac are read the way your mail is: each conversation is read once it goes
  quiet, and appointment reminders, deliveries, bills, codes and plans made
  over text land in the same folders, with an "iMessage" tag and their own
  entry under Accounts. Anything that needs doing becomes a task, and the
  task's banner opens the conversation. It needs Full Disk Access for
  Anjadhe (System Settings › Privacy & Security), which covers the whole
  Messages database, so the switch is off until you turn it on. Your texts
  go only to the model you chose; with a cloud brain they stay on this Mac
  unless Settings › AI › Cloud privacy allows Messages (a new class, off by
  default). Turn it on for one Mac. Turning it off forgets the
  conversations it read; tasks stay. The assistant still never answers a
  text.
- **Focus mode.** While a Pomodoro focus phase runs, the titlebar shows a
  focus pill with the task and time left (the way back to the timer from
  any page), the left nav folds, and email, text and routine notices are
  held and released as one summary when the phase ends. Reminders always
  come through. The Pomodoro page was rebuilt around the session: the task
  you are on, a parking lot for stray thoughts, and "While you focused" on
  the break.
- **One save indicator.** The task sheet, project sheet, Notes and Journal
  share one indicator (Unsaved / Saved / Not saved and why), and leaving a
  page saves what you typed. A new task that cannot be saved, or a blank
  title, gets a warning with a way to fix it instead of silently losing
  the text.

## Changed

- **Email AI is now called Insights.** Same page, same folders, same app
  id, since it holds what your mail and your texts said. The home card is
  "Needs a look".
- **The task detail is one sheet everywhere.** The Tasks pane, the
  Projects pane, the Calendar and the Insights doors all open the same
  card: a breadcrumb naming where you came from, the timer and pills in
  its head, hairline blocks for Linked, Decisions, History and Advanced,
  and the ask pills in its foot. The project sheet, the Projects rail
  (with a progress track under every project), the More apps page, the
  About page and the Notes rail joined the same kit. Settings is one
  680px column with every section page as a card.
- **A new app icon.** A bold rounded "A" whose legs meet at a roof, one
  indigo dot where the crossbar would be, on a deep charcoal tile.
- **News topics are a page, not a dialog.** Following a topic takes
  effect at once; the refresh runs when you leave.
- **Apple Notes carry their folder as a tag.** Imported notes are tagged
  with the Apple Notes folder they came from, alongside "apple-notes".
- **The assistant can do everything the Calendar page can with Apple
  events**: repeat presets, editing this or all occurrences, moving an
  event to another calendar, and deleting an occurrence and the ones
  after it.

## Fixed

- Switching tasks inside the first second after typing no longer drops
  the keystrokes, and a recurring task no longer loses its repeat when the
  editor is re-embedded.
- The attend-event pass on emails is now covered by the Email cloud
  privacy class like the other passes.
